## 1. HOW to design a site

### 1.1 Process (universal, mostly adopt)
- **Five approval gates, never unsupervised end to end** (claude-code-build-method.md): research, plan, structure, copy, build. Pause for human approval after each. After build, ask "did we learn anything that should update the wiki?".
- **Design craft skill `site1-design-craft`** (design-craft.md): nine hard gates, each leaving a file (facts, design brief, references, three concepts, locked tokens, screenshot review, audit + fact audit, slop test), checked by `tools/check-gates.py`. Eval verdict "partly works" (craft preferred 4 of 5 pairs, lost B2B software; 5 clients, model judges, CSS-only imagery; owner blind vote still pending). Gate 8 = fact audit; gate 1 item 9 = referral disclosure.
- **Brief before pixels** (CDM s4, vibe-coding-workflow.md): redesign vs new first; push back on vague answers ("modern and clean", "everyone"); **build around the proof that exists today** (two testimonials means plan two, no empty sections); enough-to-start checklist = what business does in one sentence, specific customer, primary CTA, differentiator, tone, required colours/fonts, launch pages, homepage sections with content status. Brief output: positioning, voice (we are / we are not), identity constraints, 3-5 style keywords with do/don't, pages with section order, homepage section table, content inventory, CTA strategy.
- **Concepts**: 3-5 directions differing on 4-5 of 10 axes (density, type treatment, weight play, visual metaphor, hero treatment, rhythm, shape, elevation, proof style, whitespace). Colour alone is not a difference. Locked across concepts: positioning, voice, CTA strategy, anchor colours/fonts. Names 2-4 words, evocative. Course says do this once per trade reference template, not per client; for a one-off real client, do it once (CDM s3).
- **Refine pass** (creative-director audit, prompt2): foundations first (type scale 5-7 sizes, colour: dark hover shade, light tint, true neutral not pure black, accent for CTAs only), then per section at desktop + 375 px judge nine things (layout, type, spacing, depth, colour, micro-details, motion, density, mobile). Verdict per section REFINE or REBUILD. Site1 addition: a conversion check per section (where is phone/form, above the fold on phone, one filled call visible at a time). "Flat cards on white = #1 sign of AI-generated design"; "centered heading + card grid = junior work".
- **Six-principle bar** (CDM s2) in Site1 form (DR K3): one recurring motif in >=5 places (cheap inline SVG), authored motion limited by B3-B5, layout tension, type as a tool, micro-details, rhythm with >=1 inverted section. Tone for trade/trust sites: "professional/trust" with one warm detail.
- **Build mechanics**: never edit `main`; branch, Cloudflare preview URL, click through the whole preview, merge only after checks pass; commit often; private repo; deploy early on workers.dev to confirm the host works; plan mode for forms; state viewport in prompts; after mobile-only fix verify desktop unchanged; re-run refine pass before token lock (it can change palette/fonts). Content collections for repeatable page types (services, areas), `status` frontmatter. Lock tokens (7 colour tokens: primary, secondary, accent, bg, surface, text, muted) before pages. No hard-coded hex/fonts in components.
- **Review process DR K1-K7**: build from brief + tokens, never blank prompt; ban lorem ipsum; run guards then screenshots at 390 and 1440; score six-principle bar; walk WebGen nine flaws; **appearance score 1-5 only to compare two versions of the same page, never a pass mark**; **a person must look at it on a real phone (no source replaces that)**; record before/after + decision in decisions-log.
- **WebGen nine flaws** (webgen-bench-notes.md s6) as review checklist: load failure (3 types), only background renders, missing UI components, image not displaying, stock template with no customization, misplaced content, inappropriate background colour. "More than half of task errors = failing to launch or failing to modify the template." Rule candidates R-W1..R-W7 (atomic acceptance tests, reject <3 appearance, verify launch first) are undecided. Its accuracy numbers are 2025-model, do not cite.
- **Anti-slop**: D5 avoid blue/purple gradients, reflex cream+terracotta, glow on every surface. Video-derived anti-patterns (video-design-notes.md): vague brief returns template layout; invented stats/testimonials/team; placeholder sections left (blog cards with no blog); main button label that promises booking but opens email/phone; two equal primary buttons; heavy/video heroes; mobile checked only by window resize; unsafe tooling habits. Stitch pilot lesson: AI design tool invented service copy while claiming "zero invented facts"; use only as idea sketch, run gate 8 on anything rebuilt from it.
- **Rejected/not-used**: scroll-hijack, scroll-scrubbed 3D, autoplay hero video, cursor effects, hover-reveal team facts, WebGL/Lenis, dark neo-brutalist/mono-body agency looks (not for call-first pages).

### 1.2 First screen (DR section A) -- phone first: design 390x844, test 375x667 and 358x667
First-screen priority order when it does not all fit (DR A preamble): (1) filled call button with number; (2) gas line (A7) and CO line (A8) where they apply; (3) H1 + trade-and-area sentence; (4) license line (in header, never leaves first screen); (5) fee line (A6); (6) one text link; (7) trust chips (A5). Header counts against the 667 px; nav collapses to labeled "Menu" if needed.
- **A1** first phone screen: trade+area, one specific H1, one filled call button (`tel:`), one text link, license line. Caveat: with a working online scheduler the text link may be "Book online". **For a medical practice: "call" may not be the dominant action; "Book appointment" likely is. This is the single biggest design-model mismatch (see s6).**
- **A2** at most one filled call element per viewport at every width; sticky bar counts; form submit and (estimate/tune-up pages only) the sticky page action are allowed second filled controls. Demo itself violates A2 on tablet/desktop and on home sticky bar (DR N).
- **A3** (proposed) hero never full-viewport with nothing below; let next section peek; never write "scroll down". Basis NN/g Illusion of Completeness (H).
- **A4** H1 names the visitor's problem or service in plain words, never a brand slogan; trade+area in H1 or sentence under it; business name in header.
- **A5** quiet trust chips: license, insured, rating with source. Rating only from `reviews_summary` (rating, count, source, date); real record with no summary shows **no rating block at all**; "insured" only when certificate seen. Placeholder rating only on demo record (`is_sample: true`), never ships.
- **A6** state service-call/diagnostic fee near the call block (LSA advertisers: same words as LSA profile). Trades-specific; medical analogue would be self-pay / insurance-accepted statements (no vault guidance).
- **A7/A8** gas-smell and CO-alarm safety lines (leave building, 911 first, never "call us first"; utility number by territory, 911 plain text not `tel:`, gas line never styled as a button). Trades-specific; the principle (emergency instruction before sales CTA, sourced wording only) transfers: a medical site needs "if this is an emergency call 911" (not in vault; see s6).

### 1.3 Motion (B)
B1 never hijack native scroll (NN/g, H). B2 scroll-driven effects only if short, below fold, desktop only, vertical only, no important text, call stays reachable. B3 motion never delays call/form; no preloader, intro, typewriter, autoplay video, carousel, marquee. B4 allowed: fades + 8-16 px reveals, CSS `animation-timeline` inside `@supports`, non-linear hover curves; decorative motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; never animate/hide H1, call button or LCP element; reveal targets visible with JS off. B5 one signature scroll moment per page. B6 no WebGL/canvas/Lenis on call-first pages.

### 1.4 Typography, colour, imagery, layout
- **C1** (proposed) two families with real contrast (serif vs sans, condensed vs wide); optional mono for numerals only. Conflict: R12a says serif+sans "reads editorial, not contractor". Demo pair Bricolage+Hanken (both grotesque sans) fails C1; owner undecided. **C3** candidate OFL pairs: Newsreader+Hanken Grotesk, Bricolage+Public Sans, Barlow Condensed+Barlow, Gloock+Figtree (Newsreader+italic+Hanken ~154 KB, over the font budget). **C4** H1 72-96 px desktop / 40-46 px phone, body 18/16-17 px, line-height 1.55-1.65, 60-68 chars/line, `clamp()`, spacing multiples of 4, rem. **C5** left-align anything >3 lines and every form; center only short headlines. **C6** no eyebrow micro-labels/index numbers/mono tags on home page.
- **D1** (proposed) 4-5 colours: ground, ink, one accent, one muted neutral secondary, tints. **D3** every pair WCAG AA 4.5:1 (3:1 large); text over photos on a scrim computed per palette (palette A example rgba(12,26,34,0.65) = 4.92:1; use 0.72 = 6.30:1). **D4** accent only for actions; nothing decorative uses it. **D6** dark and light themes both pass D3. D2 colour = brand fit, not conversion science (design look cited in 46.1% of credibility comments, 2002 study).
- **E1** real photos beat stock ("users ignore stock photos of generic people", NN/g 2010). **E3 never** stock people as "our crew", invented names/reviews, AI images with people; AI images without people only disclosed (credits block + IPTC `trainedAlgorithmicMedia`). **E4** one white balance across images; AVIF/WebP; LCP image in HTML, sized, not lazy, `fetchpriority=high` (only the true LCP at that viewport). **E5** real text, never text in images. **E6** drop decorative images or make them carry a fact. **E7** (LSA-specific) keep a client photo library with no AI edits; since 2 Aug 2026 LSA bans AI-edited/generated images in New York State; no phone numbers/addresses/screenshots in LSA photos.
- **F1** grid, alternating section tones with >=1 inverted, 64-96 px section gaps; F2-F4 optional depth/glass/motif/grid break, never at cost of speed or call; F5 group by proximity, don't reinvent standard patterns.
- Hierarchy (WDS S5/S6): emphasise with size, weight, colour, start small; headline 100% opacity, subhead ~70%; spacing in scale of 4, start generous then remove; design with real content not lorem; "good design is as little design as possible".

### 1.5 Navigation, forms, performance, accessibility
- **G5** visible top nav with Home link and text labels; on phones <=4 items visible only if call + priority items still fit, else labeled "Menu" button; sticky menus need background; touch never depends on hover. Footer must carry useful links (S8 #48).
- **G2** forms ask least: name, phone, ZIP/service need; 3-5 fields common target (vendor, L), but one case lost conversion when fields were cut; track qualified leads not submissions. **G3** visible labels never placeholder-only (Baymard H), left-aligned, validate on blur not while typing, wide buttons, clear success message. **G4** tap targets >=44x44 px (WCAG 2.2 AA needs 24; inline-in-sentence links exempt). **J7** `autocomplete` tokens (`name`, `tel`, `postal-code`), `type="tel"`, one phone input.
- **I1** CWV at p75: LCP <=2.5 s, INP <=200 ms, CLS <=0.1 (the "2.0 s" claim is not on web.dev). Lab proxy before launch (TBT for INP); field RUM after (Cloudflare Web Analytics, disclosed in privacy policy). **I2** first phone load <=1 MB, JS <=200 KB, hero image <120 KB, fonts ~100 KB total WOFF2; no video in first screen at any width. **I3** fix slowness, never a preloader. **I4** images at build time (Astro `<Image>`); Cloudflare Polish not free; Free-plan transformations fail past 5,000 (error 9422). **I5** Lighthouse mobile: gate on median of 5 runs; no current baseline. **I6** no session-replay/heatmap (e.g. Clarity) until counsel clears (wiretap-suit risk). Pre-launch also: Lighthouse mobile >=90.
- **J1** WCAG 2.2 AA; J2 reduced motion; J3 no autoplay sound/carousel/marquee/hidden scrollbar; J4 outline button border >=3:1; J5 `scroll-padding` equal to sticky bar/header height (2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured); J6 contact help in the same relative order on every page (3.2.6); J7 above.

### 1.6 Stack and build spec (astro-cloudflare-build-spec.md)
Astro 7.4.2 needs Node 22.23.4+ (use Node 24 LTS); Astro 7 breaks generated templates (stricter compiler, `compressHTML` strips inline spaces, Vite 8): put the Astro version in CLAUDE.md, run `astro check` + build after every batch. Static output needs no `@astrojs/cloudflare` adapter. Tailwind 4 via `@tailwindcss/vite`; use plain `@theme` (per-site overrides) not `@theme static`. Self-host fonts (`@fontsource-variable/*`), never a Google Fonts `<link>`. `<Image>` with width/height, quality 80, hero `loading=eager fetchpriority=high decoding=sync`, everything else lazy; compress photos; images in `src/assets` not `public/`. `content-visibility:auto` only on heavy below-fold sections. SEO integrations: `@astrojs/sitemap`, `astro-robots-txt`, `astro-seo`, `astro-seo-schema`; set live `site` URL first; default OG image required; `/design-system` page noindex. Workers custom domain needs the domain on Cloudflare DNS (else Pages). Static asset requests free/unlimited; 100,000/day applies to Worker invocations (form code). Turnstile Free: 20 widgets. Secrets in gitignored env; r2.dev is dev-only.

## 2. HOW to convert visitors to leads

### 2.1 Core model
- **Lead-site playbook "ten rules"** (lead-site-playbook.md): (1) product is leads; **fix capture and response before buying traffic**; form leads in D1 as one system of record; (2) every fact from `business.yaml`, null means omit; (3) no fabricated reviews/staff/addresses/licenses/years/stats, trust blocks render nothing when empty; (4) never promise rankings, lead counts, AI citations; (5) upgrade in place before rebuilding a site that already ranks; (6) service-area sites show no street address, schema has `areaServed` no `address`/`geo`; (7) every public form fails closed and delivers to two destinations; (8) real number primary on listing, treat every call as all-party recorded; (9) NYC contractors show DCWP license in ads, no financing content; (10) $500 build does not fund post-launch work.
- Lead-gen vs brochure: clear offer, capture mechanism, social proof, speed, follow-up system, measured conversion (research-lead-data-sources.md s6).
- A lead = call, form or estimate request, tracked by source and device.

### 2.2 Tactics with rule IDs
- One filled call per screen (A2), sticky bottom call bar that hides when another call/form/footer is on screen (G1). **The sticky bar is a project design choice; no study backs it** (G1 basis); competitors had it on 0 of 8 Queens HVAC sites. All "click-to-call lifts 20-250%" numbers are agency claims (UC).
- Phone number first and biggest, form second; "what is wrong?" row (no heat, no AC, leak) as a short path to call/form (competitor-teardown suggestions, not measured); hours and service area in first screen; fee stated; license visible and checkable (G7).
- **H4** one verb and one label per action everywhere ("Call [number]" with number in label; estimate and booking each keep one label). Compact "Call" without number in sticky bar is an open owner question.
- **H3** specific beats generic: real neighbourhoods, fees, license numbers. Trust copy answers DCWP complaint fears (quality of work 300, non-delivery of goods 244, billing disputes 164, non-delivery of service 164, unlicensed 162 of 2,536 complaints): written estimate, fee up front, license shown + checkable.
- **H6** only real reviews, dated, naming platform, never a face that isn't the reviewer's; no real reviews = skip section. **H1** concise, scannable, objective copy (NN/g 1997, old). **H2** no hype/unbacked superlatives; "24/7", "same-day", "licensed and insured", "free estimate", arrival times only when a record key backs them; benefit claims need provable facts; named guarantees only if client offers in writing, limits next to the name, written terms linked (counsel question open).
- **H7** FAQ: Google stopped FAQ rich results from 2026-05-07 and removed docs 2026-06-15; write FAQs for visitors/AI answers; `FAQPage` markup optional; never promise FAQ stars. (on-page-seo-and-schema.md still says "limited"; DR is newer.)
- **H8** translated page/line only if the answering line can serve that language (Queens: Spanish, then Chinese; 2018 ACS data).
- **H9** area pages only for areas truly served with unique local proof + 800+ words, else one consolidated area page. 
- Page structure (page-architecture.md): client site = page per real service/GBP category, nav <=6 items, URLs stable forever (301 on change), nesting up to 3 levels allowed; Home 1200-2000 words, service 800-1200, location ~800 (playbook proposes 1,000-1,500 floor, not adopted), estimate <300, FAQ 3-8 Q&A reworded from People Also Ask, one CTA per paragraph. Best-converting page is often a specific secondary service, build it well. Microsite model (40-80 flat pages, 20-30 location pages) is rejected for us (RL C1).
- **On-page SEO** (on-page-seo-and-schema.md): title `{Service} in {City}, {ST} | {Brand}` ~60 chars; meta <160 chars; exactly one H1, no skipped levels; first paragraph answers "do you serve me, how fast" in a liftable 40-60 words; editorial in-content links (multiplier claims are practitioner); JSON-LD generated from the record, most specific `LocalBusiness` subtype, `Service`, `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages, phone as text, `hasMap`, `openingHours`; no self-serving `AggregateRating`; Rich Results Test zero errors; OG/Twitter tags; WebP, descriptive alt; check `robots.txt` for accidental AI-bot blocks (Cloudflare default blocks training crawlers); entity consistency (name/service/town identical everywhere, `sameAs`).
- **GBP floors** (client sites): 2-4 secondary categories (up to 9), 20-30 services, 750-char description, 20+ real geotagged photos (playbook proposes dropping geotag floor), website-to-GBP NAP character-for-character match, turn off GBP "automated calls", do not change name/address/category/URL on a ranking listing without reason. Seasonal category rotation (HVAC example). Up to 20 service areas within ~2 h drive.
- **Call tracking & routing** (CT): events `phone_click`, `form_submit` (success only), `booking_complete`, `directions_click`. Default (PR9, proposal): real number primary on listing and static in HTML/schema/footer; tracking number as additional listing number; swap visible links by script for non-organic sessions only. WhatConverts $30/mo = single account (unlimited needs $500+ agency plan; earlier "$30 unlimited" was wrong); CallRail ~$50/mo. n8n lead flow: identify source, route, spam-filter, **do not delete flagged leads, human reviews daily**, send client email with recording/transcript; 10-second Wait before looped writes (429s); n8n fine for prototyping, add safeguards for mission-critical intake.
- **Forms** (CT, compliance): name, phone or email, service, message; client+server validation; honeypot + Turnstile; deliver to **two** destinations; show phone number on failure; webhook URL is public in client code; **swap n8n test URL to production URL before launch** or it silently dies; lead-source field ("how did you hear about us") useful for attribution.
- Speed to lead: "5 minutes" is an operating target only (the 21x figure is misquoted B2B data, UC).
- Other levers named but evidence weak: persistent mobile click-to-call, dedicated landing pages for paid traffic; vendor benchmarks 2-4% median visitor-to-lead, top 8-12%, Unbounce 2.6% home-improvement median (all vendor claims).

## 3. EDGE CASES and how they are handled

(Playbook Appendix H "edge-case register" holds more but is outside the vault files read; only items present in knowledge/compliance are listed.)
- Service-area business (no storefront): no street address anywhere; `areaServed` only; footer shows phone + service area. If client record has a real public address the client wants shown and serves customers there, template can publish it (`address.publish`, default false). **Conflict for plumbing**: NYC Admin Code 28-408.6.2 may require a business address in the identification block (counsel item O.1-O.6); until answered, no street address.
- No real reviews: skip section; real record with no `reviews_summary`: no rating block, not even a placeholder. Demo records only carry labeled "Sample" reviews (`Sample reviewer`, platform "Sample", never Google/Yelp, never realistic names; demo violates this).
- No client photos: SVG illustration, equipment photos under a shared grade with logos cropped, public-domain local architecture (NYPL, Wikimedia with credit), duotone; never caption as "our" units/jobs; AI images without people only if disclosed.
- No license number / insurance cert: omit the claim; "licensed and insured" wording never without real numbers/certificate seen.
- Competitor-has-no-privacy-policy market: gap is an opportunity, not a cost of entry (footer-and-legal-pages-notes.md: 4/8 no privacy link, 7/8 no terms, 8/8 no accessibility statement, none shows license number, fee or lookup link).
- Cookie/consent banner covering the call button: open gap; Petri's banner covers its Call button; American HVAC stacks cookie + chat + review badge. Banner text and policy must match (Petri's do not).
- Phone number wraps across two lines on phone (Michael's HVAC) -> hard to tap; keep number unbroken.
- Auto-opening chat, stacked overlays: rejected.
- No-JS fallback: sticky bar shows via `<noscript>` and creates two filled calls (demo deviation, owner undecided). Reveal targets must be visible with JS off (B4).
- Failed form submit: show phone number; never silent. Test webhook vs production URL swap.
- Duplicate content: workers.dev URL stays live after custom domain; disable it or rely on canonicals; set Astro `site` to final domain, re-check canonicals + sitemap.
- Sitemap may omit service pages: check page count against sitemap.
- Redesign of a ranking site: upgrade in place, don't rebuild (playbook 4.6, rule 5); lower DNS TTL 48 h before; export DNS zone; rollback plan; don't change name/address/URL on ranking GBP.
- Client won't move DNS to Cloudflare: host on Pages not Workers.
- Area page without unique proof: fold into one consolidated page (RL C1, H9).
- Language: translated pages only if callers can be served (H8).
- Lead quality: spam filter + human daily review of flagged queue before discarding; define "qualified lead" in writing (e.g. call >60 s) for any pay-per-lead deal.
- Seasonal demand: order area pages and time content by demand data (NYC 311 heat complaints peak Dec/Jan).
- Safety content: gas/CO lines use sourced facts only; territory unknown -> show 911 + leave instruction, no utility number.
- LSA constraints: fees in same words as LSA profile; photos without phone/address; no AI-edited images in NY.
- Content rule: sample/placeholder content must never reach a published page; production check refuses placeholders (template legal stubs are "SAMPLE TEXT" and cannot ship).
- Pre-launch placeholder sweep: no `{{...}}`, `[PHONE]`, TODO, lorem in built HTML; no `r2.dev` URLs; no Google Fonts link; no secrets/webhook test URLs in repo; `/design-system` noindex.

## 4. Hard rules / red lines / never do

### 4.1 Red-lines table (RL; IDs as in file)
- **C1** no thin city-swap pages (location page only with unique local proof + 800 words, else one consolidated area page). **C2** anchors 70% branded / 20% geo-variation / 10% exact max. **C4** no paid Reddit upvotes. **C5** no bought guest posts (chambers/real sponsorships fine). **C6** no invented/borrowed/vacant-suite address; service-area sites show phone + service area only. **C10** no traffic/CTR bots. **C11** no hidden `display:none` entity text. **C12** no broker-made GBP listings; check suspension history.
- Fake reviews, invented team members, AI "team" photos shown as real: reject.
- Course sub-agent brief tells agents to write "plausible" named testimonials/stats (B12 F23): **strip those lines**; fake-looking placeholder proof must never reach a published page.
- Do not hold a client's listing, domain, site or phone number hostage; do not reroute a non-payer's calls (proposed 2026-10-24, owner confirmation pending, PR14): client owns the listing. Domain registered to client with registrar lock, MFA, auto-renew.
- Never skip permission prompts for Claude (owner rule). No aged-domain 301s, parasite SEO, EMD farms. No interlinked microsite network disguised as independent.
- Microsite honesty: every lead goes to a real named business that knows; site must not claim to be a contractor or hold a license; referral disclosure page + "We connect you with a local licensed [trade]"; no fabricated "20 years" or crew photos. "If a course says 'we are entrepreneurs, take the risk', stop and check."

### 4.2 Other hard rules (from DR/other files)
- Never "call us first" for gas/CO (A7/A8); 911 plain text not `tel:`.
- Never a license the client doesn't hold (A1); never a "from $X" price not honoured (A6).
- No financing widgets, lender logos, "apply for financing" (NYC HIC); no referral-reward promos without counsel.
- No ranking promises, no FAQ-stars promises, no lead-count promises.
- Never ad pixels before consent; no session replay until counsel clears.
- Do not collect SSN, card or driver's-licence numbers on a lead form (SHIELD Act safeguard) -- **for a medical client this extends to any health information on forms; see s6**.
- Never raw AI draft; >=20-30% human edit heaviest in first third; banned phrases ("delve", "embark", "look no further", "cutting-edge", "one-stop shop", "second to none", "top-notch", "we pride ourselves", "we're passionate about", etc.); specific claims over generic ("fast service" -> real response time); drip-publish rather than dump (600 articles never indexed).
- No Google Places content stored beyond `place_id` (lead-finder only; not relevant to client build).
- Do not run OpenOutreach (security-audit-openoutreach.md: vendor data upload by default, unverified TLS on SMTP/IMAP, plaintext secrets, auto-publish supply chain, prompt-injection-to-email, CSV formula injection). Verdict: not with real mailbox/prospect list. Irrelevant to a client site build.
- Agent hygiene: give agent only needed access; secrets in gitignored `.env`, never pasted in chat; install skills only after review/scan; `playwright-cli` not Chrome MCP; Drawbridge-style tools reviewed before install.

## 5. Compliance traps (CC, UC, red-lines, DR O)

All "as of 2026-10-30, counsel review needed". Trades-specific vs universal flagged.

### Universal (apply to a medical site too)
- Pages: Privacy policy, Terms, Accessibility statement, Disclaimer, Complaints policy (course set); each needs licensed NY attorney sign-off; legal-policy templates are CC0 but written for apps with accounts (adapt: keep scope/info collected/how used/disclosed/cookies; drop user content/payments). Competitor privacy pages: 1,000-1,500 words, numbered sections, visible "last updated", exact lead-form data list, call-tracking/recording notice, texting consent, retention, children, NY governing law, contact route beyond phone.
- **Tracking/cookies**: no NY statute requires a cookie banner (none found); "no pixels before consent" is a California CIPA precaution and prudence rule; cookieless analytics, call tracking by number swap, disclose all tracking in privacy policy. (For a health site, ad pixels are far riskier than this note implies; see s6.)
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1, keyboard, focus, labels, alt, targets >=24 px; Site1 uses 44). Overlay widgets are partial only. NY is the highest-volume venue for website-accessibility suits (vendor counts). Send liability-limit clause questions to counsel; don't paste clause text from a course call. Include accessibility statement with contact route beyond a phone number and a demand-letter response plan (counsel's).
- **Reviews**: Google bans incentives and selective solicitation (stricter than FTC). FTC 16 CFR Part 465 (effective 2024-10-21) bans fake/AI-generated reviews, review suppression, sentiment-conditioned incentives; up to $53,088 per violation. Ask every customer the same way, no incentives, no scripted wording.
- **SMS**: A2P 10DLC registration before any texting (unregistered traffic silently dropped; needs EIN, live privacy + SMS terms); two separate consent checkboxes (marketing, project updates); FCC one-to-one consent rule vacated 2025-01-24 but revocation "by any reasonable means" stands: honour STOP and any plain "don't text me"; send 9am-8pm recipient time (planning rule).
- **Email**: CAN-SPAM, opt-outs within 10 business days, up to $53,088 per email.
- **AI voice agent/avatar**: NY GBL 396-b (effective 2026-06-09; $1,000 first / $5,000 repeat) requires conspicuous disclosure of a "synthetic performer" in an **advertisement**; unclear whether it covers an inbound phone assistant (CT says disclose on the call as best practice). An AI voice that **calls leads back** is an outbound artificial-voice call needing prior express consent (FCC 24-17): add a consent line to the form. Recording/transcription: treat every call as all-party, include recording notice.
- **Data**: NY SHIELD Act safeguards; breach notice to NY residents within 30 days. Secrets in gitignored env, debug off in production.
- NY GBL 349/350 deceptive/false advertising; insurance/warranty claims only if supplied and provable.
- **Session replay** (Clarity etc.): banned pending counsel (California wiretap suits; the cited law-firm source returned 403 so no law stated).
- **Photos shown as client's work**: ban is project red line; FTC 465 covers reviews not photos; deceptive-advertising reach of stock/AI photos is a counsel question.
- **Guarantees**: FTC 16 CFR 239.2 speaks of products; applicability to service guarantees open.

## 6. What would be WRONG or RISKY for a medical practice client

None of these are addressed anywhere in the vault; each is a flagged gap, not a rule.
1. **HIPAA / PHI**: no vault coverage. The vault's stack (n8n webhook -> email + Sheet/CRM "two destinations", Cloudflare D1 as system of record, call recording + transcripts emailed with recording links, AI voice receptionist, Turnstile, GA4, Cloudflare Web Analytics, call tracking vendors like WhatConverts/CallRail, Vapi/ElevenLabs) would route patient inquiries (symptoms, conditions, insurance) through vendors with no BAAs. Do not reuse the lead-routing/voice/tracking pipeline for medical forms or calls without BAAs or by removing PHI from forms. Contact forms should not ask for symptoms/diagnoses; consider "call us" for clinical questions. (CC says "do not collect SSN, card or driver's-licence numbers"; health data is not mentioned.)
2. **Ad pixels / tracking on health pages**: vault's "no pixels before consent" is a prudence note; for health sites, HHS/FTC enforcement on Meta/Google pixels and NY health-privacy laws make this a hard issue. Session replay (I6) must stay off. Cookieless analytics only; consider whether even Cloudflare Web Analytics and GA4 events (`form_submit`, `phone_click` tied to a service page) are acceptable on pages implying a condition.
3. **Call-first model**: DR is built around the emergency tradesperson buyer (filled call button #1, form second). Medical sites usually lead with Book appointment / patient portal / new-patient path; emergencies must say "call 911" (the A7/A8 pattern of sourced safety wording that goes before sales CTAs is the right analogue, but no medical wording exists). A2's "one filled call" and A1's license-line logic need re-deriving. The DCWP/HIC license line (A1, G7) is irrelevant; medical analogue is NYS licensure/NPI (verify via NYSED Office of the Professions lookup; not in vault).
4. **Claims and testimonials**: H2's "no unbacked superlatives", "provable facts" and FTC 465 apply, but health-specific advertising rules (NY Education Law professional misconduct for misleading advertising, specialty/"board certified" claims, outcome claims, before/after, patient testimonial rules, HIPAA authorization for patient reviews/photos) are not in the vault. "Best", "fastest", "guaranteed" already banned in the demo `claims.forbidden` list; extend with "cure", "best doctor", outcome promises. Patient photos/testimonials need written authorization; "real photos of real work" (E1) cannot be patient photos without consent.
5. **Reviews**: H6 and Google's no-incentive/no-selective-solicitation rule still apply; additionally a practice cannot confirm or reply with PHI to a reviewer. Rating chip only from real `reviews_summary`; no AggregateRating markup on own site. Health providers also see Google/Healthgrades/Zocdoc norms not covered here.
6. **Staff/credentials**: RL bans invented team members and stock "crew"; a medical site needs real clinician names, credentials, license numbers, headshots; the "skip the section if none" rule is wrong for a doctor's page where it's expected, so get real photos/bios.
7. **Medical content and SEO (YMYL)**: nothing on E-E-A-T, medical review of content, citing sources, disclaimers ("not medical advice"). The playbook's "provable fact in the record, null means omit" model fits but condition/treatment pages need clinician sign-off. FAQ rich results gone (H7). Content AI rule (>=20-30% human edit) is a floor; for medical, clinician review is needed.
8. **Schema**: LocalBusiness subtype list in vault is trades; medical would use `Physician`/`MedicalClinic`/`MedicalBusiness` (not in vault, verify at schema.org). Service-area/no-address rule (C6) does not apply: a practice has a physical address, NAP must match everywhere (the "show address only when it serves customers there" rule in G6 supports showing it).
9. **SMS/voice/AI**: A2P 10DLC and TCPA consent doubly sensitive with patient data; appointment reminders have their own rules; AI voice receptionist capturing symptoms is a PHI path. Recording notices: all-party.
10. **Accessibility**: even higher exposure; keep J1 etc. Accessibility statement + patient accommodations.
11. **Microsite/lead-gen model (referral fees, per-lead pay)**: for health care, fee-splitting/patient-brokering and NY referral rules could apply; the vault's pay-per-lead/rev-share economics must not be pitched. Do not reuse lead-selling business model.
12. **Language**: H8 (translated pages only if the line can serve callers) is directly useful; medical practices have language-access obligations too (not in vault).
13. **Emergency-sourcing pattern**: take A7's method (sourced fact, wording order, no hand-typed numbers, 911 plain text) but source it from NYC Health/DOH/practice policy; no 911 wording is sourced in vault except gas/CO.
