# Reference-site candidates (research phase)

Date: 2026-10-30. Scope: reference sites for a 2-doctor family medicine + pediatrics practice (Queens + Long Island). Multi-language out of scope. No screenshots taken (owner's pipeline captures later).

## IMPORTANT limits of this pass (read first)
- The search tool returns summarised result lists, NOT live Google SERPs, rankings, Maps packs or traffic numbers. "Evidence of traffic" below is therefore INDIRECT (appeared repeatedly in local-query results, or a large known org) and is labelled. No Similarweb/Semrush/Ahrefs figure for any candidate was obtainable; the one Similarweb hit was not usable. All traffic statements = "estimate, unverified" or "appeared in results".
- Every local query was dominated by directories (Zocdoc, Vitals, WebMD, Healthgrades, MediFind, Solv, job boards), then health systems (NYP, Mount Sinai, NYU Langone, Northwell, Catholic Health). Independent small-practice sites barely surfaced. Finding true independent organic winners needs a manual SERP/Maps check (recommended, see end).
- Several sites returned HTTP 403 to scripted fetches (bot protection): they exist (they appeared in search) but were not analysed. Marked "unreviewed".
- Design scores are inferred from fetched page structure/text, not seen visually. Re-score after capture.

## Per-site notes (included)
1. **NYU Langone Pediatric Associates Garden City.** Six pediatricians, birth to 20, English/Spanish plus interpreters. Repeated "Find a Doctor & Schedule" button, phone 516-248-8334, same-day sick visits, forms and MyChart login, doctor cards with headshots. Closest peds model to a small Long Island office, though branded by a health system. Insurance and reviews shallow.
2. **NYP Medical Group Queens Pediatrics.** Ranked first for "pediatrician Queens NY" results seen. Location cards with phone per site, "Find a doctor/location". Weak on insurance and new-patient steps: a gap the client site can beat. Value: what an organic winner looks like on this query, and its heavy mega-nav.
3. **Tribeca Pediatrics.** 50+ offices, "zero concierge or membership fees", accepts most plans and managed Medicaid, sliding scale, "Register today", info sessions, same-day sick and virtual visits to 10PM, weekends. Strong new-patient messaging; no testimonials; no sticky call bar; phone only in footer.
4. **Nao Medical.** 11 clinics incl. Astoria, LIC, Jackson Heights, Jamaica, Hicksville, Mineola. "Walk-in, book online, or virtual", phone in header and on every clinic card, ZIP/neighbourhood clinic finder, claims "200,000+ 5-star reviews" (vendor claim). Best multi-location chooser and booking prompt.
5. **ACP.** Insurance search filter, provider search, same/next-day appointment link, "Preparing for Your Appointment", portal. Site is in English, Chinese, Spanish but language is out of scope. No phone on home; no reviews.
6. **Summit Health.** "Find primary care" filtered to new patients accepting, "Request an appointment" via athenahealth, award badges for credibility. Utilitarian, conversion-path heavy.
7. **Parsley Health.** Clear membership pricing ($150/mo or $1,500/yr insured), insurance eligibility check, objection-handling FAQ and comparison against concierge/primary care. Relevant if the client offers any membership or self-pay option.
8. **Tia.** Four-step first-visit flow (coverage, care type, book, plan), "See if you're in network", visible prices, cash price list. Cited stat "90% satisfaction" is the company's own claim.
9. **One Medical.** Not fetched (403). Included as the standard digital-first benchmark (membership, app booking, pediatrics); named in several design roundups (secondary sources only). Needs capture-time review.
10. **Carbon Health.** Not fetched (403). Zocdoc case study (vendor claim) says Zocdoc brings 1,500+ new-patient bookings per month, suggesting integration with a marketplace booking widget. Review at capture.
11. **Zocdoc.** Not a style model; the competitive reality. City/specialty/insurance pages owned nearly every local result. Study the insurance-first filter and "accepting new patients" signal; consider a clinic-profile there.
12. **Pediatric Associates (FL).** 95+ offices, Healow "Book an appointment" on every card, "Today's hours" per office, new patient guide, walk-in and after-hours positioning. Good office-card pattern for a two-office practice.
13. **Northern Virginia Pediatrics.** Single-office design-roundup pick: full hours up front, separate New Patient and Newborn registration, "What you need to know before your first visit", selected testimonials, AAP symptom checker, urgent nurse line, transparent form fees.
14. **Dr Yanlun Li Family Medicine.** Real Flushing solo family doctor that appeared in the local query. Photo slider, Contact Us, phone/text/WeChat contact, walk-ins accepted. No insurance info, no reviews shown, no booking. Shows the competitor baseline the client can beat.
