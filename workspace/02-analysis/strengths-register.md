# Strengths register (what to adopt, and how to adapt without copying)

Date: 2026-10-31. Same sources and limits as flaw-register.md (text + static screenshots; no traffic or user-test data). Site keys: FF base, ACP, MSC, NAO, NVP, NYP, NYU, OM, PAR, PAFL, SUM, TIA, TRI, YAN; premium: BB, CAS, CITY, CLT, GS, LO, ORAL, ROCK, SFP, TJ.

Adaptation rule (counsel has not cleared lookalikes): we adopt STRUCTURE and IDEAS only. No copied layout, wording, imagery, icon sets, brand colours, logos, slogans or code. Every row's "How we adapt" is written so the result is our own composition from our own facts. Evidence graded: observed = seen in capture/HTML; claim = vendor/company statement; judgement = my craft reading.

## Strengths we deliberately do NOT adopt
- Membership/concierge framing, large national "stat bands", awards carousels, and per-service photo cards (not our model or not provable).
- Mascot-led or yellow/cyan brand systems (BB, ROCK): wrong for an adult + family practice and a contrast risk.
- Booking via a third-party scheduler in v1 (adds trackers and BAA work).
- Reviews widgets, until live GBP data and consent exist.
