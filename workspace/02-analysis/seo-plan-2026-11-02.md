# SEO plan (2026-11-02), from the Site1 playbook (U18, U06, page-architecture, medical-wellness overlay)

## What the built site exposes today (dist/, checked 2026-11-02)
- All pages: `noindex, nofollow` + robots.txt `Disallow: /` (correct while this is a demo; both come off at launch only).
- No canonical tags, no sitemap, no Open Graph tags, no JSON-LD on any page.
- Titles are unique (good). Descriptions are short and generic on inner pages ("Meet the two doctors of the practice.").
- Services exist only as a section on Home (10 services in 2 groups, `serviceGroups` in practice.ts). No service has its own URL.
- 2 real locations with their own pages (allowed by the playbook), 2 doctor pages, contact, legal.

## The problem (the owner is right)
One page can rank for one main query group. Today everything about "well-child visits", "vaccines", "school physicals", "annual physicals" lives inside Home, so Home competes for ten different searches and wins none. Playbook: U06.2 "one page per primary offer", page-architecture "service page per real service / GBP category", hub and spoke (Home -> Services hub -> each service, each linking to both locations and the doctor).

## What NOT to do (playbook)
- NOT 10 services x 2 towns = 20 near-identical pages (U18.14 doorway abuse; page-architecture rejects thin city-swap pages).
- NOT pages written only to rank (U18.15 scaled content abuse), NOT unreviewed health claims (medical-wellness.5: provider reviews and evidence-backs claims), NOT AggregateRating/Review markup (U18.11), NOT invented credentials, hours or "accepting new patients" (project red lines).

## Blocked on the client (cannot be faked)
Real domain, real addresses/phones/hours, doctor credentials, doctor-reviewed service text, Google Business Profile ownership, whether online booking/portal exist (affects nav and schema).
