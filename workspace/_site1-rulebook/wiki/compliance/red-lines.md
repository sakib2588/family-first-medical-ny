# Red lines

The course teaches some tactics that break Google or platform rules or deceive customers. The owner already rejected most of them on the earlier SEO project (2026-09-19, MASTER_REPORT §1). They apply here too.

## Microsite-specific honesty rules

- Every lead goes to a **real, named business** that knows it is receiving the lead.
- The site must not claim to be a contractor, or to hold a license, that it does not. Include the referral-marketing disclosure page and a clear "we connect you with a local licensed [trade]" statement where the site is not the contractor.
- No fabricated "20 years in business" or crew photos. Use the actual contractor's facts when leasing to one.
- If the course says "we are entrepreneurs, take the risk" about a tactic, that is a signal to stop and check, not to proceed.
