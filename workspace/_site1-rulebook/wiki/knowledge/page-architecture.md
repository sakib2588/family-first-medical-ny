# Page architecture and URL rules

Two models exist. Pick by site type.

## A. Microsite (course blueprint, B06 F04, blueprint wiki)

- 40-80 pages: 8 core (Home, About, Services, Service Areas, Contact, Estimate, Thank You, FAQ) + about 9 supporting/legal (Meet the Team, Careers, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer, Complaints Policy, Accessibility, Referral Marketing Disclosure) + **1-6 service pages** (1-2 works best; more services = next site) + **20-30 service-location pages**.
- **Flat URLs**, all slugs at root. Internal linking, not nesting, shows authority. Pattern for location pages: `[service]-in-[city]-[region]`.
- Hub and spoke: homepage is the main ranking target; Services links to every service; Service Areas links to every location; location and service pages cross-link to siblings and nearby areas.
- Header: Home, Services dropdown, Service Areas, About, Contact, Get an Estimate, persistent Call Now (`tel:`).

## B. Client site (GBP kit, file 03)

- Page inclusion by rule, one keyword to one URL, no duplicate primary keywords. Service page per real service/GBP category; location pages **only** for real staffed locations, or for service-area businesses that can supply area-specific proof.
- Nesting up to 3 levels allowed; URLs stable forever (301 if changed).
- Nav <= 6 items; click-to-call visible in the mobile header.

## Conflict and resolution (location pages)

The course blueprint mass-produces 20-30 templated location pages. The owner-approved decision on the earlier project (C1) rejected thin city-swap pages in favor of one consolidated 1,000-1,500 word geographic page, and the kit calls near-identical city pages doorway spam. The course's own Claude Code house rules agree: **real local specifics per location page (housing stock, streets, jobs) or skip the page** (B11 F04).

**Rule for us:** build a location page only when it carries unique local proof and 800+ words of non-templated content. Otherwise fold the neighborhood into one consolidated service-area page.

## Page contents and limits (course)

Home 1200-2000 words; service 800-1200; location about 800; estimate under 300; FAQ 3-8 Q&A sourced from "People Also Ask", reworded not copied. One CTA per paragraph. Hero block: H3 subline, H1, call token.

## Best-converting page

Often a specific secondary service, not the flagship (B06 F04). Build that page well.
