# Design rules for lead sites

One numbered rule set for every Site1 page. It merges [[web-design-source-notes]] (S1-S8), [[course-design-method]], [[webgen-bench-notes]], the playbook research R8, R9, R10, R11 and R12 (`playbook/_research/`), the web checks of 2026-10-29, the gaps-D finds merged on 2026-10-30 (log: `playbook/_research/R13-parts/review/gaps-merge-log.md`), the round 2 review fixes of 2026-10-30 (log: `playbook/_research/R13-parts/review/fix-log-2.md`), and one extra fix pass after round 3 on 2026-10-30, not re-graded (log: `playbook/_research/R13-parts/review/fix-log-3.md`). Each rule has a **basis** (where it comes from), an **evidence** level (Ev.) and an **enforce** column.

Status: **reviewed with open issues** (both round 3 reviewers returned FAIL; the fixes after round 3 are not re-graded). Rules marked *proposed* settle a conflict from [[web-design-source-notes]] section 9 on the evidence below; the owner can overrule any of them. Practitioner numbers follow [[unverified-claims]]: use them to decide, never to quote to a client.

Scope: this rule set is the trades lead-site overlay of the Universal Website Design Playbook (research brief R14 delivered 2026-10-29; module split pending); the universal modules are being defined separately ([[decisions-log]] 2026-10-29).

- **Statutes and rules** cited in this file are written as of the date given, counsel review needed; none of them is a finding that a site meets a law (section O).
- **Evidence:** **H** = primary source read (web.dev, NN/g, W3C, Cloudflare docs, FTC, DCWP, utility pages); **M** = several consistent practitioner sources, or our own measurement of a small sample; **L** = a single, vendor or practitioner source, or a project decision with no study behind it. "Old" marks a study from before 2015. The level grades the evidence, not the authority: a rule from [[red-lines]] or an owner decision binds even at L.
- **Enforce:** **G** = a guard or test can check it. Where a check exists today it is named (paths under `demos/kiln-and-coil/`); "no guard yet" means a guard could check it but none exists. **R** = a reviewer or a person checks it.
- **Scope tags:** **[client site]** = the site is the contractor's own. **[microsite]** = a referral site that passes leads to a named contractor ([[microsite-model]]); it must never read as the contractor or claim a license it does not hold ([[red-lines]]). Rules without a tag apply to both.

Skills to use for each job are in `.claude/skills/site1-skill-router`.

## A. The first screen (phone first: design at 390 x 844, test at 375 x 667 and 358 x 667)

The test sizes are the ones the demo already uses (`e2e/qa-home.spec.ts`); they still ignore browser toolbars, so K6 (a real phone) stays required.

First-screen priority (phones). When the A rules do not all fit at 358 x 667, keep these inside the first screen in this order and move the rest below the fold, starting from the end: (1) the call button with the number, the one filled call of that screen (A2); (2) on the home page and on every page A7 or A8 covers (gas-appliance, heating and water-heater pages, the no-heat guide), the gas line (A7) and, where A8 applies, the carbon-monoxide line (A8), both always inside the first screen; (3) the H1 and the trade-and-area sentence; (4) the license line (A1), placed in the header so it never leaves the first screen; (5) the fee line (A6); (6) one text link; (7) trust chips (A5). The header (logo, nav, call) counts against the 667 px; on phones the nav collapses to a labeled "Menu" button if visible links (G5) would push item (1) or (2) below the fold. Guard: no guard yet (extend `e2e/qa-home.spec.ts` to check items 1 and 2 at 358 x 667).

## G. Forms, calls and navigation

| # | Rule | Basis | Ev. | Enforce |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Phone first. On phones a sticky call bar appears only when no other call button, page action, form or footer is on screen, and hides again when one is, so it never makes two filled call buttons visible (A2). ## K. Review process (how a page is accepted)

1. Build from the brief and tokens, never from a blank prompt. Ban lorem ipsum; use real content.
2. Run the guards (G rows), then a screenshot pass at 390 px and 1440 px.
3. Score the six-principle bar from [[course-design-method]] in its Site1 form: motif (F3); motion as B3-B5 allow it, with at most one signature moment instead of the course's three or more animation types; no stat counters unless the number is in the business record with a source; no motion that runs on its own on call-first pages (anything that starts automatically and lasts more than 5 seconds needs a pause control, WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2, Level A); eyebrows per C6; layout tension, type as a tool, micro-details and rhythm as the course has them. Check D5's list (no blue-purple gradient, no reflex cream plus terracotta pair, no glow on every surface) and record pass or fail.
4. Walk the nine flaws from [[webgen-bench-notes]]: loads, only background renders, missing components, missing images, stock template with no customization, misplaced content, inappropriate background color.
5. Score appearance 1-5 only to compare two versions of the same page (graders disagree on level but agree on ranking, [[webgen-bench-notes]]). The score is never a pass mark. A version is accepted when it passes the gates (step 2, with the call button in the first phone screen) and a person has looked at it on a real phone (step 6).
6. A person looks at it on a real phone. No source we have replaces that.
7. Record before-and-after screenshots and the decision in `decisions-log`.

## O. Open counsel questions (counsel review needed, as of 2026-10-30)

Questions for counsel and the owner, not rules. Nothing here says that a site meets a law, and no requirement or red line is dropped here. Statute text comes only from sources opened on 2026-10-30.

- **Plumber identification block, NYC Administrative Code 28-408.6.2.** Text as opened 2026-10-30 on a public-domain mirror (https://nycadmincode.readthedocs.io/t28/c04/art408/; it shows no currency date, and the official American Legal Publishing copy returned 403): "All business vehicles, advertising, websites and stationery used in connection with a master plumber business" must prominently show the licensee's full name, the words "N.Y.C. licensed plumber", the license number and the licensee's business address; a trade, partnership or corporate name goes immediately above the licensee's name. Questions:
  1. Is the mirror text the current code?
  2. **Real client address [client site].** Red line C6 ([[red-lines]]) says service-area sites show no street address, and Google tells a service-area business to remove its address from the Business Profile when it does not serve customers there (H9 source). Must a plumbing client's site show the business address on its license, and if so, how does the owner square that with C6 and with a profile that hides the address? G6 and this counsel item stand together under G6's rule, not as contradictory rules: until counsel and the owner answer, a service-area plumbing site shows no street address (red line C6).
  3. **Fake, borrowed or vacant-suite addresses.** Not open: such an address is never used, whatever counsel says about the code (red line C6, [[red-lines]]).
  4. **Referral microsites [microsite].** Is a microsite that passes leads to a named master plumber advertising used in connection with that plumber's business under this section? If so, whose name, license number and address must appear, and where, and how does that sit with A1 and G6 (the license shown only attributed to the contractor) and with the referral disclosure ([[compliance-checklist]])?
  5. Does "prominently" fix a place on the page (first screen, footer, every page)?
  6. **License line by trade (A1).** NYC 311 lists plumbing as work that does not need a DCWP HIC license (KA-01808, opened 2026-10-30). Which license line must a plumbing site show in the first screen and footer: the DOB master plumber license with the 28-408.6.2 block, and also a DCWP HIC number for any non-plumbing home-improvement work the client advertises?

  Until counsel answers, a reviewer marks every plumbing site "counsel item open: 28-408.6.2" (R). The identification block on a site is never treated as a pass mark. Known gap: plumbing sites have no launch gate pending counsel; whether a plumbing client site may go live while these questions are open is for counsel and an owner decision in [[decisions-log]].
