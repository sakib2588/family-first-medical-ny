# U20. Legal, privacy, consent and claims

Status: draft | Owner task: T7 | Updated: 2026-10-30 | Scope: universal

## Purpose
This module answers which legal pages a site needs, what consent posture it runs, and which rules govern reviews, endorsements, claims, AI disclosure and children's data. It runs after the content and proof steps (see U05, see U07) and before QA (see U23). Every row is a pointer for counsel, written as of 2026-10-30, counsel review needed. It is not legal advice and it never concludes that a site meets a legal requirement; only counsel signs off rows.

## Anti-patterns
- A cookie banner whose "accept" is pre-selected while the analytics tag loads on first paint. Consent comes after the tag, so it is no consent; gate the tag (U20.4, U20.5).
- A privacy policy copied from another site that names trackers the site does not run and misses the ones it does. Generate the tracker list from the build, then send to counsel (U20.2, U20.8).
- A "reviews" carousel of unattributed five-star quotes written by the builder. This is a fabricated review and a red line; show real, attributed reviews or no section (U20.11).
- Results testimonials ("saved thousands") with no typical-result proof. Remove or add the generally expected result, with counsel (U20.13).
- A support chatbot styled as a named human agent. Label it as AI at the first message (U20.15).
- A footer line saying the site follows all privacy laws. That is a legal conclusion no builder can make; write what counsel signed off and when (U20.22).

## How an AI builder uses this
The builder reads `site.yaml` (`jurisdictions`, `archetype`, `model`, `legal.triggers`, `legal.data_collected`, `analytics.tools` with `sets_cookies`, `legal.ai_disclosure`, `legal.audience_min_age`), applies each row that begins `When jurisdictions includes <slug>:` only when that slug is in the record (U00.18) and every row with no condition to every client (U00.19), the proof library from U05 and the event map from U19, then merges the matching jurisdiction and vertical overlay rows into a per-client compliance checklist with a counsel column. It produces the list of required pages, draft legal pages marked "draft for counsel", the consent gate configuration, the tracker inventory for the privacy policy, and a claims audit that links each claim to a proof item. It must refuse to publish fabricated or unattributed reviews, results claims without proof, AI people shown as real, children's data collection without counsel sign-off, and any sentence stating that the site meets a law. It never fills a statute number, threshold or deadline that is not in an overlay row with a source opened in that session and a counsel flag.
