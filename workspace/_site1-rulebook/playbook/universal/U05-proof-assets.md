# U05. Proof, assets and rights

Status: draft | Owner task: T7 | Updated: 2026-10-30 | Scope: universal

## Purpose
This module answers what real proof the client has today (reviews, case studies, licenses, credentials, awards, press, data, photos, team), with what permission, how each item is verified and how it is shown. It runs after intake (see U02) and the brief (see U04), and before the sitemap (see U06) and copy (see U07), because sections are planned only around proof that exists. Its outputs are the proof library (`proof.*` in `site.yaml`) and the rights log; the client-facing request is `playbook/kit/asset-and-proof-request.md`. Legal points are pointers for counsel, written as of 2026-10-30, counsel review needed.

## Anti-patterns
- A generated page ships with "key statistics", a community rating and three testimonials nobody supplied. That is fabricated proof; delete the blocks and keep the sections out of the plan until proof exists (U05.2, U05.3, U05.6).
- A placeholder reviews block "to be filled later" goes live. Placeholders leak; the sample label scan and null-means-omit templates stop it (U05.5).
- An AI-made before-and-after pair with an invented job story. Show only real, dated, consented work (U05.14, U05.17).
- Images lifted from an inspiration board on the theory that AI pictures are free. Provenance cannot be checked, so the asset has no rights row and cannot ship (U05.16, U05.18).
- A star rating badge with no platform or date beside the call to action. Show the platform, link and date, or nothing (U05.3, U05.4).
- Logos of publications under "as seen in" for a paid advertorial. Label it paid or drop it (U05.13).

## Checklist
- [ ] Proof inventory complete before S2; every planned proof section maps to a record item (U05.1, U05.2).
- [ ] Trust markers read from verified record fields; none rendered from empty data (U05.3).
- [ ] Every review has platform, link, date and reviewer as shown; no edits beyond marked trims (U05.4).
- [ ] Built pages scanned: no "Sample" label, placeholder proof or unsourced figure (U05.5, U05.15).
- [ ] Relationship and incentive question answered for every review and testimonial; disclosures placed beside quotes; counsel review needed (U05.6, U05.7).
- [ ] Review exclusion criteria written and applied to all reviews; counsel review needed (U05.8).
- [ ] Case studies signed off; each number has source, period and baseline; typicality reviewed with counsel (U05.9).
- [ ] No self-serving review-star markup (U05.10).
- [ ] External review profiles linked (U05.11).
- [ ] Each credential links to its registry with a how-to-check line; expiry recorded (U05.12, U05.19).
- [ ] Award, membership and press claims match their evidence (U05.13).
- [ ] Team photos and names consented; no AI or stock people shown as real (U05.14, U05.17).
- [ ] Rights log has a row for every deployed asset and quote (U05.16, U05.18).
- [ ] Re-verification booked for launch and T+90 (U05.19).

## How an AI builder uses this
The builder reads `site.yaml` (`proof.*`, `assets`, rights log), the brief from U04 and the returned `playbook/kit/asset-and-proof-request.md`. It writes the proof library and rights log, marks each item verified or not with the checker and date, and hands U06 and U07 the list of sections that real proof can support. Templates render proof only from verified record fields and omit a section when its data is null. The builder must refuse to write, generate, rewrite or "improve" any review, testimonial, rating, statistic, credential, team member or before-and-after image; to fill a proof section with placeholder content on a client site; to add star markup for self-serving reviews; and to deploy an asset without a rights row. It merges the vertical overlay's trust-signal list and the jurisdiction overlays' review and endorsement rows, flagging each for counsel (see U20).
