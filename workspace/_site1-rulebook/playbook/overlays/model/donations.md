# Overlay: model/donations

Status: planned | Owner task: T8 | Updated: 2026-10-30

## Trigger
The client record's `model` list contains `donations` (slug from STRUCTURE.md): the site takes one-time or monthly gifts (usually `nonprofits`); monthly giving also raises the `subscription` questions. Planned (U00.7): the build proceeds with the universal modules and the vertical overlay; these questions go on the counsel list, and launch (S6) waits for recorded counsel review or an owner waiver in the handoff pack.

## Primary conversion
Defaults are not set; launch waits on the questions below (U00.7). Proposed, not decided: a completed donation, one-time or monthly; secondary: volunteer sign-up, newsletter. Conversion paths: U08 (a plain "Donate" button in the first screen, a monthly option, a short form close to checkout speed, the use of funds stated next to the form). Donation events and amounts: U19. Receipts, donor privacy and solicitation disclosures: U20.

## Open counsel questions
- Receipts and acknowledgments: what the confirmation email and receipt must state for the written acknowledgment and the quid pro quo disclosure, as of 2026-10-30, counsel review needed [source: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1771.pdf, A, 2023, opened 2026-10-30] [source: https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-contributions-written-acknowledgments, A, 2026, opened 2026-10-30].
- Charitable solicitation registration: in which states does an online donate page that reaches their residents require the client to register first, and what disclosure statements must appear on the page. Counsel to identify.
- Processing fees: may donors be asked to cover them, and how are fee and net gift shown? Counsel to identify.
- Card data: whether an embedded donation form puts the client in card-payment scope; counsel and the processor to confirm [source: https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faq/articles/Frequently_Asked_Question/how-does-an-e-commerce-merchant-meet-the-saq-a-eligibility-criteria-for-scripts, A, 2025, opened 2026-10-30].
