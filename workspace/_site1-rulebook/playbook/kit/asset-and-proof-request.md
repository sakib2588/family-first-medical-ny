# Asset and proof request

Status: draft | Owner task: T9 | Updated: 2026-10-30 | Scope: universal | Serves: U05, U09, U11, U12, U13, U20

This is the list of brand files and proof we need to build your site. We build only around proof that is real today and that you have the right to use. Two real reviews make a better page than ten we cannot check.

If you do not have something, leave the field blank rather than invent it: a blank field means that section is not shown, while an invented one misleads your customers and breaks our rules.

Due date for this request: `<YYYY-MM-DD>`. Questions go to: `<day-to-day contact>`.

## How to send

- [ ] Put the files in a shared folder that your business owns (for example your own cloud drive) and invite us to it; never send a password or a link that needs one (U05, U21).
- [ ] Send original files: the photo straight from the camera or phone, the logo from the designer; not screenshots, social media downloads or images saved from messaging apps (U05, U13).
- [ ] Name each file so it matches its row below, for example `photo-front-counter.jpg` (U05).
- [ ] Fill in one rights line for every file and every quote (template below) (U05).
- [ ] If you are unsure about a field, leave it blank and tell us; we ask rather than guess (U05).

## Rights and permission: the rule for everything below

For every photo, video, logo, font, illustration, review and testimonial we need three things: who made it, who owns it, and what permission covers its use on your website and anywhere else you want it (social media, print, ads). If an identifiable person, a private home or a client's premises appears, we also need that person's or owner's written consent. Consent forms come from your counsel; counsel review needed.

**Rights line (one per file or quote):**

| Field | What to write | Module |
|---|---|---|
| File or item | The file name, or the link to the review | U05 |
| Made by | Photographer, designer, reviewer or staff member | U05 |
| Owner | Who holds the copyright or the license | U05, U13 |
| Permission | Who gave it, when, and for which uses (website, social, print, ads) | U05 |
| Consent | Written consent from identifiable people or property owners; a parent or guardian for a child; counsel review needed | U05, U20 |
| Limits | End date, places it may not be used, credit line required | U05 |
| Where used | Filled in by us | U05 |

## B. Photos and video

- [ ] Original photo files of your real work, place, products and people, with the date taken and what each one shows (U13).
- [ ] For each photo: who took it, and do you have the right to use it on the website and elsewhere (U05, U13)?
- [ ] Written consent from every identifiable person in a photo; for a child, from a parent or guardian; counsel supplies the form; counsel review needed (U05, U20).
- [ ] Written consent from the owner of any identifiable home, premises or vehicle that is not yours (U05).
- [ ] Before-and-after pairs: same subject, both dated, consent as above; we caption what was done and when (U05, U13).
- [ ] Images of patients, clients or customers in a regulated field only with the consent that field requires; see the vertical overlay; counsel review needed (U05, U20).
- [ ] Video: original files, any music license, and captions or a transcript if you have them (U13, U16).
- [ ] Stock images you already bought: the license and the source. Stock is used only for abstract or background images, never presented as your team, your work or your premises (U13).
- [ ] AI-made images: tell us which ones they are. They are labeled, and never shown as your real people, customers, premises or work (U13, U20).
- [ ] If a photo shoot is needed: who arranges it, and who owns the photos under the photographer's contract (U01, U13).

## C. Reviews and testimonials

We show reviews exactly as written. We may shorten one with "..." but never change its meaning, and we never write one. Search engines do not show review stars for reviews a business publishes about itself, so we add no star markup for them [source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet, A, 2026].

- [ ] Review text exactly as written, with the link to where it appears (U05).
- [ ] The reviewer's name exactly as the platform shows it, the platform and the date (U05).
- [ ] Permission to reproduce the text where it is needed: we ask the reviewer, or use the platform's own embed; counsel review needed (U05, U20).
- [ ] Relationship: is the reviewer staff, family or a business partner, or did they receive anything (a discount, a gift, a free product) for the review? It must be disclosed; counsel review needed (U05, U20).
- [ ] Testimonials collected directly, not on a platform: the exact text the person approved, how they want to be named, and their written permission (U05).
- [ ] Did you ask only happy customers for reviews, or reward only good ones? Tell us; counsel review needed before we show them (U05, U20).
- [ ] The reviews shown give a fair picture: nothing is hidden or filtered in a way that misleads (U05, U20).

## E. Licenses, registrations and insurance

- [ ] The public registry link where anyone can check it; we look the number up ourselves instead of copying it from memory (U05).

- [ ] Insurance: a certificate we can see. We show that cover exists, never the policy number (U05).

## How we verify proof

| Proof | How we verify | What the site shows | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reviews | We open the link and match the text, name and date, and note the check date | Text as written, name as shown, platform, link, date | `proof.reviews` with `verified` |
| Case studies | Written sign-off from the named client; the source document for each number | The period and starting point next to each result | `proof.cases` |
| Licenses and registrations | Lookup in the public registry | Number and registry link; expiry kept in the record | `proof.licenses` |
| Certifications and awards | Issuer page or certificate | Issuer and year; no wider claim than the certificate | `proof.credentials`, `proof.awards` |
| Press | We open the link | Outlet and link; "as seen in" for earned coverage only | `proof.press` |
| Team | Written consent on file | Real names and real photos only | `proof.team` |
| Photos and video | Rights line complete; consent on file | A caption saying what it shows and when | `assets`, rights log |
| Data | Source document | The source next to the number | `proof.data` |

- [ ] Every proof item has a `verified` entry (who checked it, and the date) before it reaches a page (U05).
- [ ] Anything we cannot verify stays off the site, and we tell you why (U05).
- [ ] Proof is checked again before launch and at the T+90 check, because reviews, licenses and memberships change (U05, U23).
- [ ] Expiry dates (licenses, insurance, memberships, permissions) go into the record so the site never shows an expired claim (U05, U24).
- [ ] Every file and quote has a row in the rights log: file, creator, owner, license, permission (who, when, scope), consent forms, where used, expiry (U05, U13).

## What we will not do

- Write, buy or rewrite reviews, or show invented reviewers (U05).
- Show invented or AI-made people as your staff or customers (U05, U13).
- Show an address, license, award or affiliation that is not yours (U05, U20).
- Add star markup to reviews you publish about yourself (U05, U18).
- Use a photo, logo or font that has no rights line (U05, U13).
- Present a result as typical when it is not (U05, U20).
