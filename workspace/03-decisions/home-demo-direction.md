# Home demo: design direction (skill: frontend-design-direction, 2026-10-31)
1. Purpose: let a worried or busy parent or adult patient reach the practice (request a visit or call) in one tap, and trust them within seconds.
2. Audience: parents and adults in Nassau/Queens on a phone, often in a hurry; some on slow data; some with accessibility needs.
3. Tone: calm, warm, credible. Quiet editorial restraint (hairlines, whitespace, near-zero shadow), not a cinematic dark hero.
4. Memorable detail: one arch shape device (frames photos, caps sections, shapes the logo mark), echoing AIMSS's gold line/pulse motif, used consistently.
5. Constraints: Astro + Tailwind; WCAG 2.2 AA; tap targets >= 44px; 390px phone first, then 1280px desktop; no third-party scripts; JS optional (site works without it); brand palette from aimssfoundation.org (indigo, teal, gold, cream, lavender) with teal-deep for actions.
