- Position: totally ownable theme (log cabin, lanterns, Boogaloo hand-lettered type, woodcut-style badge logos) with a second brand for tongue-tie/airway.
- Voice: campy ('Welcome to Camp Little Tooth').
- Visual: photographic lodge-interior hero with dark overlay and huge white display text.
- Offer: 7 tel links, nav; booking not obvious in fold.
- Evidence: moderate; mostly theme.
- A11y: full-width hero text white over dark overlay good contrast; but the cookie/privacy panel covers 25% of fold; accessibility floating button present (green circle).
- Pricing/insurance: none at fold.
- Mobile: H1 'WELCOME TO CAMP LITTLE TOOTH' at ~50px Boogaloo wraps over 4 lines and is cut off by the cookie panel; the fold shows two logos and a headline but no CTA.

## What makes it feel premium
A committed concept with real photography of the real space; shows what 'ownable' means.

## Risks for a medical practice
Theme overwhelms information; contrast of H1 over busy photo; fold lacks CTA; kids themes do not fit a 2-doctor family-medicine practice serving adults and tweens.

## How a 2-doctor family practice could use it
Show the real waiting room/exam room photography with soft overlay - but keep the CTA in the first fold, unlike this site.
