## Evidence per dimension
- Position: 'Care that is high touch, high trust, and high tech.' - tension-pairing line.
- Voice: plain, human; 'We'll come to you.'
- Visual: noise-textured blue-to-lime gradient hero, white light-weight grotesk 56px, outlined (not filled) CTAs, large full-bleed photos.
- Offer: 'Learn more' and 'Get support' outlined buttons; segment navigation (members, partners).
- Evidence: 'Get support' lines; limited proof above fold.
- A11y: white on saturated blue ok; outlined rectangular buttons with 2px border clear; 5 images, all with alt.
- Pricing/insurance: not in home hero (insurance-sponsored model).
- Mobile: clean; large type, 1-col.

## Visual craft analysis
H1 'national2' (Klim, geometric-grotesque) 56px weight 300, line-height 1.2; body national2 16px #1E1F42 on white/pale green. Palette: navy #1E1F42, electric blue ~#0064FA, lime ~#C8F46B gradient end, pale mint #EFF4F3. Imagery: documentary-style real people (human, specific). Radius 0. Shadows 0. Motion: gradient/noise hero static. Buttons: outlined 1-2px square, bold 16-18px with arrow.

## What makes it feel premium
Big light-weight sans on a single gradient, flat squared UI, documentary photography. Disciplined.

## Risks for a medical practice
Gradient + dark navy tone reads tech/fintech more than a family doctor; for a small practice would feel cold and corporate; not conversion-first (no phone/book in fold).

## How a 2-doctor family practice could use it
Only borrow: light-weight oversized sans heading, 2px outlined secondary button, and the paired-adjective tagline structure ('high touch, high trust, high tech'). Do not copy the gradient/tech look.
