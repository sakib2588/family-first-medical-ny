## Evidence per dimension
- Position: 'Trusted pediatric care' - generic words, but identity (yellow + cyan, bold logo) is ownable.
- Voice: friendly, parent-plain; 'Real reviews from real parents', 'Healthcare for every age, stage and phase'.
- Visual: saturated yellow hero #FFDD34 with a cut-out doctor and child, rounded pill trust strip, scalloped cloud dividers, NCQA badges.
- Offer/booking: BEST in set. First fold on phone has newborn-appointments banner, Spanish, logo, search, 'Book an Appointment' full-width pill and tappable phone number; desktop adds trust strip (Accepting new patients / Same-day / 24-7 on-call / Extended hours / Most insurance accepted).
- Evidence: NCQA Patient-Centered Medical Home and Behavioral Health Integration badges, provider quotes with real names/credentials (APRN, FNP-C), parent reviews.
- A11y: dark teal #052C33 on yellow/white = high contrast; large 14-16px labels; 22 images no alt of 151.
- Insurance: H2 'Most Insurances Accepted' plus Insurances nav link; real 'Bill Pay', 'Patient Portal' in utility bar.
- Mobile: strongest. Single-col, big buttons (40px radius pills), hamburger + search.

## Visual craft analysis
H1 'Basel Classic' 52px weight 300, tracking normal, line-height 67px (1.3), dark teal #052C33; body 'Basel Grotesk' 16px; all-in-one custom type family (serif-ish classic + grotesk). Palette: sunshine yellow #FFDD34, cyan #45CBE5 (CTA), dark teal #052C33, pale aqua #E5FAFF, mint strip ~#E4F2EC. Imagery: real kids/doctors, cut-out on flat color, patient-scale warmth. Spacing: roomy; hero 640px+; modular 12-col. Radius: pills 40px; cards 24px, scalloped cloud edges. Shadow: none (0 in DOM; flat design). Icons: solid filled friendly icons in a trust strip. Motion: carousel of provider quotes, scroll sections. Buttons: cyan pill, dark text, sentence case ~14px.

## What makes it feel premium
Flat saturated color + soft pill shapes + real photography + a trust strip that answers the 5 parent questions at once. Feels bespoke without being fragile.

## Risks for a medical practice
Yellow + cyan is loud for a family practice that wants calm authority; 151 images (heavy) and 15s load; some content is multi-location boilerplate; 'Trusted' headline is a claim not proof.

## How a 2-doctor family practice could use it
Copy the first-fold structure verbatim in concept: announcement bar (accepting newborns), logo, big pill 'Book / Call' with number visible, and a 5-item trust strip. For NY: add 'Se habla espanol' and language list if relevant. Pick one signature brand color and use cut-out/flat backgrounds for doctors.
