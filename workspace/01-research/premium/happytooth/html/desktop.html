    Our Space

    I designed Happy Tooth to specifically not feel, look, or smell like a typical dental or medical experience. Every detail is intentional and inspired by my own kids and their favorite spots at children's museums around the country. We are known for creating a welcoming, safe environment, with the goal of building a space where kiddos genuinely look forward to returning.
