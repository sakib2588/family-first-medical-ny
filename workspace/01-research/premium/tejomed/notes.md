## Evidence per dimension
- Position: 'Healthcare that feels like a relationship' / 'Your own dedicated family doctor in Portugal'; 'Fewer patients. More time.' is a crisp mechanism-based claim.
- Voice: plain, human, benefit-first; comparison table (typical clinic vs TejoMed membership).
- Visual: warm cream/deep-green palette, tidy sans, doctors as portrait cards, subtle starburst brand mark.
- Offer: single CTA 'Get in touch', 'Talk to a TejoMed advisor' floating card, FAQ, videos (cofounder 4:38).
- Evidence: '5.0 on Google (109 reviews)', language ('English, Portuguese & Spanish'), license number ERS 182003 shown in the trust strip (strong regulatory proof).
- A11y: large Cookiebot modal on first load blocks phone fold entirely (full-screen consent wall); body text dark on cream good.
- Pricing: 'What does membership fee cost' is an FAQ item; fees not on page body in capture.
- Mobile: first-fold on phone is the cookie modal; underlying layout is a stacked single column. 12.6k px page.

## Visual craft analysis
H1 'Open Sauce Sans Bold' 48px weight 700, tracking -1.5px, line-height 54px (1.1), near-black teal #102226; body Inter-like sans; 'Lightstep' sub-brand set in a serif. Palette: deep green #1C3F3A, cream #F6F2E6 and #ECE8D9, gold accent for eyebrows ~#B8860B, white. Imagery: lifestyle/in-home photography with natural light, portrait cards with starburst placeholder when no photo. Spacing: generous, alternating cream/white/dark-green bands. Radius: ~4px buttons, 12px cards. Shadow: one large soft (68px blur) on floating media card. Icons: simple line icons. Motion: floating cards, video play overlay. Buttons: dark green solid, sentence case.

## What makes it feel premium
Specific mechanism claim, licence number and review count in a single trust strip right under the fold, and calm green/cream palette with generous bands. Rarely over-designed.

## Risks for a medical practice
Full-screen cookie consent at first view (GDPR) - US practice does not need that; membership model cost hidden; weight-care add-on ('Lightstep') is promotional.

## How a 2-doctor family practice could use it
A trust strip directly under the hero: Google rating + count, languages spoken, NY medical license/NPI numbers, 'accepting new patients'. The Typical clinic vs our practice comparison grid is a clean way to explain a 2-doctor family practice (same-day sick visits, one doctor, etc.) without hype.
