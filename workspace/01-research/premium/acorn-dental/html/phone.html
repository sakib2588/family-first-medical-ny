Welcome!

Whether it’s your child’s first visit to a dentist or he/she is returning for treatment, you and your little one will receive a warm welcome and you’ll be treated with care and respect. Answers to your questions about pediatric dentistry



What happens during a pediatric dental checkup?

Our routine checkups and cleanings include a gentle exam, professional cleaning, and guidance on brushing and nutrition. These visits help prevent cavities and keep your child’s smile healthy as they grow.



When should my child be evaluated for tongue or lip ties?

If your child has trouble feeding, speaking, or shows signs of oral restriction, we recommend a tongue and lip tie assessment. Early evaluation can help determine if a simple release procedure is beneficial.



What types of fillings do you offer for children?

Our Middletown pediatric dentists use white, BPA-free fillings that blend naturally with your child’s teeth. These are durable, safe, and designed to restore teeth while maintaining a natural appearance.



What is a space maintainer?

A space maintainer is used when a baby tooth is lost early. It helps keep space open for the permanent tooth, preventing crowding or alignment issues later on.



Does my child need a sports mouthguard?

If your child plays sports, a custom sports mouthguard is highly recommended. It helps protect teeth from injury during activities like football, basketball, and more.



Do you offer emergency dental care for kids?

Yes! We provide emergency dental treatment for issues like toothaches, broken teeth, or injuries. If your child is in pain, contact us right away for prompt care.



What sedation options are available for children?

We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and IV sedation to help children feel relaxed and comfortable during treatment. We’ll recommend the safest option based on your child’s needs.



When should my child see an orthodontist?

We provide comprehensive Middletown pediatric orthodontic evaluations to monitor growth and development. Early assessment can help guide proper alignment and reduce the need for more complex treatment later.
