Our services

At Avala Pediatrics you will find a dedicated team of professionals helping parents navigate through the exciting and challenging times of child’s growth and development, from newborn to being a young adult.

Same-day Appointments

Life happens and you can’t afford to wait for a spot to open-up on a physician’s calendar. Being available to our patients is very important to us. Newborn Services

Newborns are little miracles that need utmost attention. Babies go through changes in the first few weeks that require close monitoring and guidance from the medical provider. We enjoy caring for the newborns by forging a strong bond with parents and being available for the parents at any time as needed.

Well Child Visits

Children grow rapidly during early years. At every well visit your doctor discusses the stages of development and watches for any changes to make sure corrective action is taken. If needed immediate referral to the required services is provided at the same time.

Immunizations

Also known as vaccines are important to prevent fatal communicable diseases like whooping cough, measles, mumps and chicken pox. The vaccines are administered according to CDC guidelines.

Health Education

Education is a very important part of every visit at the office as it promotes good health and optimal development. Promoting healthy behaviors in adolescents regarding healthy eating habits, peer interactions and driving by partnering a trusting relationship built on mutual trust.

Why Choose Us

Something About Us

Patients are at the heart of our practice. We strive to provide each patient with compassionate and quality care and make each child feel welcome and comfortable when they walk through the door. Our staff works hard to deliver each patient excellent service at every visit. We want you and your family to be as informed as possible about the care from the first appointment through all the stages of your child growth and development. We listen to and honor your family’s perspectives and choices. The doctor and office staff are here to answer any questions before scheduling your first visit.

Our Vision & Mission

Our mission is to provide compassionate and personalized care for infants, children and adolescents in a professional and friendly environment. We believe in prevention of disease and promotion of healthy life-styles for all children. Our physicians and staff are recognized for compassion, experience, knowledge and a commitment to excellence. We practice medicine as a team and value the contributions of all of our staff in providing excellent medical care and service.
