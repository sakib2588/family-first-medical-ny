How care actually happens at Cassia.

Define

Begin

Onboarding visit, baseline labs, your plan goes into action. Same-day access and a physician who knows you. No insurance billing for your membership. Your membership covers care with Dr. Nylander. You can still use your insurance for outside services such as labs, imaging, and specialist visits.

Meet Dr. Zazi Nylander

Dr. Zazi Nylander, MD, FAAFP, DABOM, is a double board-certified physician in Family Medicine and Obesity Medicine, a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner, and the founder of Cassia Optimal Health. Born and raised in Ghana, she saw early how compassionate, relationship-centered care can change a community, and that belief shapes how she practices medicine today.

Named an Atlanta Magazine Top Doctor in 2025 and 2026 and a Castle Connolly Top Doctor in 2026 and 2027, Dr. Nylander is also a physician educator and speaker on metabolic health, women’s health and personalized primary care.
