## What makes it feel premium
A fully ownable graphic system (shapes + palette) instead of stock photography; press proof as a wall of logos; real doctor, real space; confidence. This is the 'brand, not template' benchmark.

## Risks for a medical practice
Cosmetic-dentistry register (veneers, 'Leader') is not primary care; claims such as 'set a new standard' and 'Leader in' are puffery regulators dislike for medical practices; heavy page (25s load in capture); missing alts; no price/insurance info; press quotes are third-party assertions.

## How a 2-doctor family practice could use it
Make a simple ownable shape system from the clinic logo (e.g. circles/half-circles in 3-4 brand colors) as hero background and section dividers - zero stock photography needed, dodges the generic-stock problem, cheap for a 1-week build. Pair with one real photo of the two doctors.
