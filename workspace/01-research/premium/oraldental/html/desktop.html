Experience Comfortable Dental Care with Nitrous Oxide at ORAL Dental Studio

At ORAL Dental Studio NYC, located on the 2nd floor at 131 Essex Street in Manhattan’s Lower East Side, we prioritize your comfort and well-being during every dental visit. We understand that dental anxiety is a common concern, which is why we offer nitrous oxide sedation dentistry—a safe and effective way to help you relax during treatments.

Nitrous oxide, commonly known as "laughing gas," is a mild sedative administered through a comfortable nasal mask. It induces a state of relaxation while keeping you fully conscious and able to communicate. The effects wear off quickly after the procedure, allowing you to resume your daily activities without downtime.

Nitrous Oxide

GET A STRAIGHTER SMILE WITH LINGUAL BRACES AT ORAL DENTAL STUDIO

Orthodontic treatment does not have to change how your smile looks day to day. At our Lower East Side office on Essex Street, our team offers lingual braces for patients who want to correct alignment issues without a visible appliance. This option has become especially popular among adults balancing professional and social commitments who still want lasting orthodontic results.

Unlike traditional braces, lingual braces are attached to the back of your teeth, making them invisible when you smile, speak, or laugh. Each set is custom-made to fit the shape of your teeth, allowing for precise movement over the course of treatment. Patients appreciate the ability to pursue a straighter smile privately, without drawing attention during everyday interactions.'

Lingual Braces
