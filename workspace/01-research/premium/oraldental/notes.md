## Evidence per dimension
- Position: 'Smile, it's ORAL' pun + 'where elegance and innovative dentistry converge'; modern and clean but not very distinct.
- Voice: polished, generic luxury ('Relaxing exceptional care').
- Visual: full-bleed photo of the studio hallway with a frosted rounded-rectangle text card; a pop-up for Free Lingual Braces Consultation over the fold.
- Offer: 'Book Online' black button + phone outlined, sticky 'Open 7 days a week' bar, calendar FAB on phone; emergency same-day section.
- Evidence: Google 4.9 / 503 reviews right in the hero (strong), blog.
- A11y: grey 13px Poppins body on white (#676A6A on white ~5.3:1) acceptable; desktop nav wraps to 2 lines (clumsy), 13/50 images no alt.
- Insurance: explicit: 'We Do Not Accept Medicaid, HMO or State Insurances' on the hero + Insurance nav menu (9 link matches): honest but harsh.
- Mobile: fold has 2 sticky bars + top bar; hero card; floating calendar button - slightly cluttered.

## What makes it feel premium
Real interior photo, review-count proof in the hero, sticky hours bar, honest insurance statement.

## Risks for a medical practice
Frosted glass card over a busy photo reduces legibility; popups on first view; font-size 13px body too small; insurance exclusion shown as the second line of the hero is off-putting but honest.

## How a 2-doctor family practice could use it
Show Google rating and count in the hero; keep a persistent 'Open / Call / Book' bar on mobile. Be explicit about insurance - but lead with 'plans we accept', not exclusions.
