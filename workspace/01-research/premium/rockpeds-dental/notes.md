## Evidence per dimension
- Position: 'pediatric dentistry that rocks' - a pun that becomes a brand: lowercase wordmark, rock mascot with blush cheeks, pink/baby-blue palette.
- Voice: playful and consistent ('smiles rock. kids rock. you rock.'); 'easy-peasy'.
- Visual: sky-blue hero with wavy white divider and a smiling rock illustration holding the pink CTA.
- Offer: 'Book an appointment' pill in fold, floating phone button bottom-right, in-house dental discount plan mentioned, 'sedation-free dentistry' bullet list with colored check marks.
- Evidence: modest (checklist claims, some testimonials). No credentials above fold.
- A11y: white on pink #FF80AE for button text = low contrast (est. ~2.4:1); body grey #545454 on white ok; 15 of 19 images no alt. reCAPTCHA badge overlaps phone view.
- Pricing: 'discount plan' mentioned; insurance not at fold.
- Mobile: strong; big pill CTA, floating call button; copy centered.

## Visual craft analysis
H1 text in Poppins 500, lowercase; body Montserrat 16px #545454. Palette: baby blue #BADFFF, hot-pink CTA #FF80AE, blush #EFCAC1, grey rock #C9D0D3, dark text #545454. Imagery: custom character illustration + simple vectors. Radius: pill 50px buttons, rounded logo badge. Shadow: soft 59px-blur 10% (14 uses). Icons: colored check marks, Font Awesome social. Motion: waves, floating call button (fixed). Buttons: uppercase tracked 14px.

## What makes it feel premium
A real brand character and a lowercase wordmark with a pun. Cheap to execute, very ownable, consistent voice.

## Risks for a medical practice
Over-cute for a doctor's office with adults; white-on-pink button contrast; mascot-driven brand does not scale to a family medicine + adult patients; cartoon sedation/airway claims need care.

## How a 2-doctor family practice could use it
Idea to borrow: a mascot-free version: one ownable wordmark treatment + a voice rule (e.g. short, warm, lowercase headline phrases). A single fixed floating call button on mobile is a good pattern.
