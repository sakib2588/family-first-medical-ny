Cookies and other technologies
Cookies
“Cookies” are small text files that are stored on your computer or other mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies can be used for many purposes, including to remember an individual user as well as their previous interactions and preferences with respect to a website.
We use the following cookies on the Parsley Health website (the “Website”):
Strictly necessary cookies that are essential for you to use the Website and its features. This category only includes cookies that ensure basic functionalities and security features of the Website. These essential cookies are always enabled as without them the Website will not function properly.
Analytics cookies that are used to understand how users interact with the Website. These cookies help provide information on metrics, the number of visitors, where Website traffic originates and similar matters for this purpose.
Advertising cookies that are used to provide you with relevant advertisements or promotions based on your previous activity and interactions with the Website. To fulfill this function, these cookies collect information about you and your activities on this and other websites.
Other technologies
We use Google Analytics and Mixpanel to understand and evaluate how visitors interact with the Website and the services that we provide through it (the “Services”). These tools help us improve our Website, its performance you’re your experience, together with any services provided through our Website.
You can opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites by downloading and installing an opt-out browser plugin provided by Google that is available via this link .
