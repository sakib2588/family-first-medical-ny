Privacy Policy & Cookie Policy

Parsley Health, Inc.

At Parsley Health your privacy is important to us. Our Privacy Policy describes how and why we collect, use, share and otherwise process your Personal Information [when you use or access our Platform / Website]. This Privacy Policy also describes the choices you have with the information we collect, including how you can manage, update, or request to delete information.

Please take a moment to review this Privacy Policy. It is important that you understand this Privacy Policy so that you are fully aware of how and why we are using your Personal Information. By using our Platform, you are agreeing to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may Contact Us at any time.

About Parsley Health

Parsley Health is the leading holistic medical practice in the United States designed to help members overcome chronic conditions. We provide access to a dedicated care team, which includes clinicians trained in multiple specialties such as family medicine and internal medicine.

Parsley Health is not a medical group or a health care provider. Parsley Health provides its users with the ability to obtain a telemedicine consultation provided by independent medical practitioners including, but not limited to, Parsley Medical Group FL, P.A., Parsley Medical PC, Parsley Medical, P.L.L.C., and other affiliated practices (collectively “Medical Group”), an independent medical group with a network of United States based health care providers (each, a “Provider”). Medical Group (or your own medical provider if you do not use a Medical Group Provider) is responsible for providing you with a Notice of Privacy Practices describing its collection and use of your health information, not Parsley Health.

Identity and Contact Details of the Data Controller and Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Parsley Health is the data controller responsible for your Personal Information. For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your Personal Information, you can contact our Privacy Team using the details set out in the Contact Us section.

Personal Information

What is Personal Information?

Personal information is information from and about you that may be able to personally identify you. We treat any information that may identify you as personal information. For example, your name and e-mail address are personal information.

What types of Personal Information do we collect?

We may collect and use the following personal information (hereinafter, collectively referred to as “Personal Information”):

  • Personal Identifiers and contact details: a real name, birth date, e-mail address, telephone number billing, home, work or shipping address, or driver’s license or passport.
  • Financial information: credit card number, debit card number.
  • Health or medical information: physical characteristics or description.
  • Characteristics of protected classifications under California or federal law: Race, Sexual Orientation, gender, Color, Age, National origin, or Disability.
  • Visual Information: Photos.
  • Internet or other electronic network activity information: IP address, device mode, device ID, OS version, device language, operating system, browser type, browsing history, search history, and information regarding a consumer’s interaction with an Internet Web site, application, or advertisement.
  • Geolocation data: Physical location or movements, local time, and local time zone.

How do we collect your Personal Information?

We collect most of this Personal Information directly from you. For example, when we speak to you by phone, text message, and e-mail. Additionally, we will collect information from you when you visit our Website or App and fill out forms, use our Software, wear one of our Devices, or purchase our Services.

We may also collect Personal Information in the following ways:

  • When You Use A Premium Feature. When you choose to participate in a premium service, we collect additional information from you related to those services. Some premium features are paid services.
  • When you make payments through the Platform. We do not collect or store financial account information, though we may receive transaction identifiers and summary information that does not include credit card or bank account numbers.
  • When You Contact Us. When you contact Parsley Health directly, such as when you contact our Customer Support team, we will receive the contents of your message or any attachments you may send to us, as well as any additional information you choose to provide.

We will also collect information automatically as you navigate through our Platform via cookies and other tracking technologies. For more information about our use of cookies and similar tracking technologies, please see our Cookie Policy.

How do we use your Personal Information?

We may use your Personal Information for the following purposes:

  • Category of Personal Information
  • Personal identifiers and contact details
  • Health or medical information
  • Internet or other electronic network activity information
  • Geolocation data
  • Purpose of Processing
  • To operate, maintain, supervise, administer, and enhance our Platform or the Services (including monitoring and analyzing the effectiveness of content on the Platform, aggregate site usage data, and other usage of the Platform and/or the Services such as assisting you in completing the registration process).
  • Legal Basis
  • Necessary for our legitimate interests in order to effectively operate our business and provide you with the Services that you request from us.

How do we share your Personal Information?

We may share Personal Information with third parties in certain circumstances or for certain purposes, including:

  • Our business purposes. We may share your Personal Information with our affiliates, vendors, service providers, and business partners, including our data hosting and data storage partners, analytics and advertising providers, technology services and support, and data security advisors. We may also share your Personal Information with professional advisors, such as auditors, law firms, and accounting firms.
  • Your healthcare providers or family. With your consent, we may share your information, including information collected from your use of our Platform, with your health care providers and/or family members (e.g., immediate family or friends) that you designate to receive your information.
  • With your consent. We may share your Personal Information if you request or direct us to do so.
  • Compliance with law. We may share your Personal Information to comply with applicable law or any obligations thereunder, including cooperation with law enforcement, judicial orders, and regulatory inquiries.
  • Business transfer. We may share your Personal Information to a buyer or other successor in the event of a merger, divestiture, restructuring, reorganization, dissolution, or other sale or transfer of some or all of our assets, whether as a going concern or as part of a bankruptcy, liquidation, or similar proceeding, in which Personal Information held by us about our users are among the assets transferred.
  • To enforce our rights. We may share your Personal Information to enforce any applicable terms and conditions and Terms of Use, and to ensure the safety and security of our Services and our users.
  • De-identified information. We may also disclose de-identified information, so that it cannot be reasonably used to identify any individual, with third parties for marketing, advertising, research, or similar purposes.
  • To market our products and services. We may share your Personal Information with affiliates and third parties to market our products and services.
  • To market third party products and services. We may share your Personal Information with affiliates and third parties to market their products or services to you if you have not opted out of these disclosures. For more information on opting out, see Your Choices about how we share your Personal Information.
  • Third Party Analytics. We use Google Analytics and Mixpanel to understand and evaluate how visitors interact with our Platform and/or the Services. For more information on the use of such tools, please see our Cookie Policy.

Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, the types of cookies that we use and how to control your cookie preferences.

Cookies and other technologies

Cookies

“Cookies” are small text files that are stored on your computer or other mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies can be used for many purposes, including to remember an individual user as well as their previous interactions and preferences with respect to a website.

We use the following cookies on the Parsley Health website (the “Website”):

  • Strictly necessary cookies that are essential for you to use the Website and its features. This category only includes cookies that ensure basic functionalities and security features of the Website. These essential cookies are always enabled as without them the Website will not function properly.
  • Analytics cookies that are used to understand how users interact with the Website. These cookies help provide information on metrics, the number of visitors, where Website traffic originates and similar matters for this purpose.
  • Advertising cookies that are used to provide you with relevant advertisements or promotions based on your previous activity and interactions with the Website. To fulfill this function, these cookies collect information about you and your activities on this and other websites.

Other technologies

We use Google Analytics and Mixpanel to understand and evaluate how visitors interact with the Website and the services that we provide through it (the “Services”). These tools help us improve our Website, its performance you’re your experience, together with any services provided through our Website.

You can opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites by downloading and installing an opt-out browser plugin provided by Google that is available via this link .

How do we use cookies?

Like most other online services, the Website uses first-party and-third party cookies for a number of purposes, details of which are set out in the table in the “What types of cookies do we use” section below.

First-party cookies are cookies that are set by our Website. Third-party cookies are set by a third-party domain on our Website. The third-party cookies used on our Website are needed to serve a number of functions, including (but not limited to) providing insights on our Website’s performance, providing an understanding of how you interact with our Website, providing advertisements that are relevant to you and so on.

What types of cookies do we use?

Only navigational related cookies are used on this site.

How do I change my cookie settings?

You can change your cookie preferences for the Website at any time through the cookie settings.

In addition, each type of web browser provides ways to restrict and delete cookies. Browser manufacturers provide resources to help you with managing cookies. Please see below for more information on some popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Internet Explorer
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari (Desktop)
  • Safari (Mobile)
  • Android Browser
  • Opera
  • Opera Mobile

For other browsers, please consult the documentation that your browser manufacturer provides or visit the browser developer’s website.

Please note that choosing to restrict cookies via browser settings may, in some cases, impact on your ability to access or use certain parts of our Website and/or the Services.
