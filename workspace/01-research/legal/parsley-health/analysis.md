# Legal / footer analysis: Parsley Health (parsleyhealth.com)
Captured 2026-10-31. Not legal advice.

## Footer inventory
- Layout: dense multi-group footer (Complete Care; Clinical Lab Review; About Us; Resources; Centers: NYC, Virtual), newsletter sign-up, social (Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn/YouTube), address "126 5th Ave, Fl 2, New York, NY 10011", then a legal row: Clinical Membership Terms | Terms & Conditions | Terms of Use | Privacy Policy | Notice of Privacy Practices | Accessibility Statement.
- Copyright: "(c) Parsley Health, all rights reserved" (no year). No phone, hours or 911 line; no nondiscrimination or language-assistance link; no price-transparency link (Pricing & Insurance page exists).

## Flaws / risks
1. Five separate terms-type documents (Terms of Use, Terms & Conditions, Clinical Membership Terms, plus membership) create overlap/conflict risk and reader fatigue (about 17,000 words in terms alone).
2. Stale dates (2021) on NPP and terms; privacy updated 2024.
3. Footer copyright has no year.
4. Accessibility statement is thin (about 150 words), no WCAG target stated in text captured.
5. No Section 1557 notice found in footer.
6. Policy mentions advertising/GA; static capture found no tag scripts (scripts may be loaded by a tag manager at runtime: unverified).

## What to borrow
- Clear footer label "Notice of Privacy Practices" next to "Privacy Policy"; a combined Privacy & Cookie page; "Last Updated" on each doc. Avoid the five-document sprawl.
