Captured 2026-10-31. Not legal advice. Out of state; use for pattern only (FL patient-rights page is not NY).

## Footer inventory
- Layout: very large (118 footer links in the phone capture, nearly all office/location names, then services), language toggle "ENGLISH / Espanol", and a compact legal row: FLORIDA PATIENT'S RIGHTS & RESPONSIBILITIES | NOTICE OF PRIVACY PRACTICES | PATIENT REFERRALS | SITE MAP | WEBSITE PRIVACY STATEMENT; "(c)2026 Pediatric Associates".
- No NAP in footer (per-office pages), no hours, no 911 line, no Section 1557 / accessibility / terms of use link. A vaccination policy page exists but is not in the footer.

## Flaws / risks
1. Website privacy page duplicates its content several times (page assembly bug): 19.5k extracted words and 4 copies of the California section for a Florida practice.
2. Typo in the privacy contact email in the California section ("pedatricassociates.com", vs pediatricassociates.com elsewhere): wrong-address risk for rights requests.
3. California-heavy text (32 mentions) for a Florida business: generic template feel.
4. "Updated 2026" but "effective 2020": reader cannot tell what changed.
5. No terms of use, accessibility, nondiscrimination notice in footer; vaccination policy unlinked.
6. Footer is 118 links on mobile.

## What to borrow
- NPP with dated effective date, headed sections and a short "Your rights / Complaints / Contact us" structure; a patient-rights page with a grievance route; a vaccination policy statement (pediatric-specific); a language toggle in the footer.
