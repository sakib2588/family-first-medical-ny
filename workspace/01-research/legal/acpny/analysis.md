# Legal / footer analysis: AdvantageCare Physicians (acpny.com)
Captured 2026-10-31. Not legal advice. Nearest NYC primary-care group with a full legal section.

## Footer inventory
- Layout: 4 columns (About Us / Our Providers + borough links / For Patients / ...), then a legal strip: Legal | Nondiscrimination Policy | Privacy and Security Policy | Accessibility Statement | Language Assistance; myACPNY portal link. Header has Espanol and Chinese toggles.
- Footer links to dedicated "Medical Records & Privacy", "Billing", "Insurances We Accept", "Support/Contact".
- No address/hours/phone in footer, no 911 line, no credential line, no copyright line captured; social icons (Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube) present.

## Flaws / risks
1. Website privacy gap: no cookie / tracking policy while the page loads Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook) pixel, DoubleClick, Criteo and Bing ads tags (verify with counsel; regulator focus on health-site pixels).
2. 2018 date on the privacy document (8 years old). Accessibility page says WCAG 2.0 and mentions Twitter (stale).
3. Policy labelled "Privacy Policy" is really an NPP stub (title/content mismatch).
4. No dates on nondiscrimination and accessibility pages.
5. Footer has no NAP/hours/emergency line.

## What to borrow
- Legal hub + dedicated /legal/ path; a separate, plain-language "Medical Records & Privacy" page; multilingual NPP; named complaint routes on the nondiscrimination page.
