Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

You are accessing a digital platform, including a website, mobile application, or other online interface (each a "Digital Platform"), that is owned, operated, or controlled by Nao Medical (referred to in this Privacy Policy as "Nao Medical," "we," "us," or "our").

This Privacy Policy applies to all Digital Platforms that display or reference this Privacy Policy. It also applies to all related online services, tools, and features that we provide or make available through those Digital Platforms, including but not limited to interactive tools, widgets, plug-ins, downloadable materials, applications, content, and other online functionality (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy governs how we collect, use, maintain, disclose, and otherwise process information obtained through the Services when such information is not considered Protected Health Information ("PHI") under applicable healthcare privacy laws. The policy applies regardless of the method used to access the Services, including through desktop computers, mobile devices, tablets, or other electronic means.

If you submit information through the Services for the purpose of receiving healthcare services from Nao Medical, certain information you provide may qualify as Protected Health Information ("PHI") under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA") and its implementing regulations.

Our collection, use, and disclosure of PHI are governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices (the "HIPAA Notice"). The HIPAA Notice explains how PHI may be used or disclosed by Nao Medical and outlines your legal rights regarding your PHI under applicable federal and state law. This Privacy Policy applies only to information that is not PHI. In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between this Privacy Policy and the HIPAA Notice with respect to PHI, the HIPAA Notice will control and govern.
