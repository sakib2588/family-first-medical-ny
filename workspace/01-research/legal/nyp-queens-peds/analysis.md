# Legal / footer analysis: NewYork-Presbyterian Queens Pediatrics (nyp.org)
Captured 2026-10-31. Not legal advice.

## Footer inventory
- Layout: newsletter sign-up block on top, then 4 link groups (About NYP, Ways to Help, Professionals & Students, Job Seekers), 4 social icons, then a legal bar.
- Legal bar: Disclaimer | Privacy Notice | Price Transparency | Website Privacy Policy | Employees | "(c) 2026 NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital".
- Missing from footer: accessibility statement, nondiscrimination / language assistance, patient rights, telehealth, SMS terms, cookie settings link. No NAP, hours, or 911 line in the footer.
- Newsletter checkbox: "I accept NewYork-Presbyterian's Terms and Conditions" and opt-in to marketing; the "Terms and Conditions" link goes to /privacy-notice (same URL as Privacy Notice), so there appear to be no separate site terms.

## Topic coverage (keyword)
Website policy: collection, use, sharing, third parties, cookies/ads, security, rights (limited), opt-out, "Your Consent" up front. Not found: retention, children, breach, HHS/NY AG complaint, language access.

## Readability
Website policy Flesch about 14; Privacy Notice reads easier but is very short.

## Flaws / risks
1. No dates on privacy documents (staleness cannot be judged by a visitor).
2. "Terms and Conditions" label pointing to the privacy notice (duplicate-URL footer link; no real terms page found).
3. No accessibility or nondiscrimination link in the footer of a NYC hospital system (language access is a NY expectation; verify with counsel).
4. Policy says consent by use ("Your Consent" heading): browse-wrap consent for a health site is weak (verify with counsel).
5. Privacy language names Google Analytics and advertising while the page runs Google Analytics only (policy broader than page: acceptable direction of error).

## What to borrow
- One-line separate "Privacy Notice" (HIPAA) vs "Website Privacy Policy" labels: clear split in the footer. Avoid: mislabeled Terms link, missing dates.
