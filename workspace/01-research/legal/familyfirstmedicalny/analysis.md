# Legal / footer analysis — familyfirstmedicalny.com (BASE SITE)
Reviewed 2026-10-30 by agent a70cba5e (summary text), written by main session. Not legal advice. Every row below is a checklist item to verify with counsel; nothing is declared compliant.

## What the site has
- Footer: tagline, 2 addresses + phones, 4 in-page anchor links, copyright line, "Board-Certified Family Medicine & Pediatrics · Medicaid, Medicare & most insurances accepted".
- 11 legal-style URLs probed (privacy, terms, notice of privacy practices, HIPAA, accessibility and others): all genuine 404.
- No trackers, cookies, forms or third-party scripts except Google Fonts, so privacy exposure is low today and rises the moment forms, chat or analytics are added.
- Security headers: HSTS/CSP absent.

## Content risks noticed
- Self-served AggregateRating + Review JSON-LD for 5 reviews (policy risk; our template bans it).
- "Safe. Effective." graphic and efficacy wording in the IV section with no risk text.
- "Open 7 Days" shown globally while Hicksville is closed Wed/Thu.
- "Since 1998 / 25+ years" and board-certification claims unverified (one physician's residency reportedly ended 2025).

## Our improvement (to design later)
Ship all of items 1-6, 8, 12, 13, 20 as real pages, linked from every footer; dated, with owner contact; wording from counsel, never copied from other sites.
