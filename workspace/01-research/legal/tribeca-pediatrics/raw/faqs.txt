Newborn Care      
        What tests and screenings are given in the hospital?
After your baby is born, several important screenings, medications, and vaccinations are performed to ensure a healthy start. Your hospital team will review each of these with you before discharge and answer any questions you may have.
Screening Tests
• Hearing test
• CCHD oxygen test – screens for congenital heart disease
• Newborn screen test – checks for rare but serious conditions
• Jaundice assessment – measures bilirubin levels (transcutaneous or serum)
Medications and Vaccinations
• Vitamin K injection – prevents spontaneous bleeding in newborns (only offered in the hospital)
• Erythromycin eye ointment – protects against bacterial eye infections
• Hepatitis B vaccine – first dose given shortly after birth
• Beyfortus (RSV protection) – offered seasonally (October through April)
        When should I schedule my first newborn appointment?
You can register your child today, even if your baby is not yet born.
For newborns, we recommend scheduling their first pediatric appointment within 3-5 days after birth. Simply give us a call, and we’ll work with your schedule to ensure the best care for your family and your new baby.
For older children, we offer same-day sick visits to address urgent medical needs. For well-child visits, we encourage families to send us their child’s medical and vaccine history before the appointment. Don’t worry if you don’t have access to prior medical records. Our team is happy to help you, so your child receives the care they need without delays.
        How does newborn insurance work?
Before delivery, contact your insurance provider to confirm newborn coverage and learn how to add your baby to your policy.
After birth, your baby is typically automatically covered under the parent’s insurance for the first 30 days. During that time, be sure to formally add your baby to your plan to ensure coverage continues without interruption after the initial 30-day period.
        Will my Tribeca Pediatrics pediatrician come to the hospital?
Your baby will be cared for by the hospital’s pediatric team during your stay. Once you’re discharged, our team at Tribeca Pediatrics will take over your baby’s care for all follow-up visits and ongoing support.
Medical Care & Services       
        What services does Tribeca Pediatrics offer?
We provide comprehensive pediatric care, including newborn care, well-child visits, same-day sick visits, vaccinations, developmental screenings, and virtual consultations.

        What happens during well visits?
During routine well-child visits, our pediatricians focus on your child’s growth, development, and overall health. Each visit includes a comprehensive physical exam, discussion of nutrition and sleep, and age-appropriate guidance to help your child thrive.
We also monitor key developmental milestones — including motor skills, language, and social-emotional progress — to ensure your child is reaching important stages at the right pace. 
Before your visit, you’ll receive an email outlining what to expect, so you can come prepared and informed.

Vaccinations & Health Policies       
        What is Tribeca Pediatrics' vaccination policy?
Tribeca Pediatrics adheres to the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) vaccine schedule, ensuring your child receives the safest and most effective immunizations available, and all our vaccines are Thimerosal-free.
At Tribeca Pediatrics, we are strong advocates for vaccination and take a compassionate, educational approach to address vaccine hesitancy. Our goal is to build trust with families and increase vaccination rates by fostering open, nonjudgmental communication.
We do not discharge patients who decline vaccines but instead focus on education and encouragement to help families make informed health choices.
        Do you provide travel vaccinations for kids?
Yes! We offer travel vaccinations (Typhoid and Yellow Fever) and health consultations at select locations for patients planning international trips. We also offer pre-travel vaccine appointments. 
        Does Tribeca Pediatrics provide flu shots and seasonal vaccines?
Yes! We offer flu shots, COVID-19 vaccines, and the RSV shot (when applicable) to keep your child protected year-round.
