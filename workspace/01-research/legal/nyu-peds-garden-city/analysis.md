## Topic coverage (keyword scan, approximate)
Website privacy covers: collection, purposes, disclosure, third parties, cookies, analytics/pixel, security, opt-out, HIPAA vs website distinction. No explicit: retention, children, breach, HHS OCR/NY AG complaint route (not found by keyword).

## Readability
Rough Flesch about 13 (college-professional); short sections help. Not plain-language.

## Flaws / risks
1. Accessibility overlay widget (UserWay / accessiBe strings in the captured HTML) while publishing an accessibility policy: overlays are contested as a compliance claim; verify with counsel.
2. Price Transparency opens on a different domain (med.nyu.edu): fine for users, but a second brand/domain to keep current.
3. Several policy pages show no date (accessibility, public notices, hub).
4. Policy shell pages are ~275-290 KB of HTML each (heavy for mobile) vs ~18 KB for the standard-charges page.
5. Hospital-scale content; little transfers directly to a small practice.

## What to borrow
- Dedicated legal bar separate from navigation; accessibility help line inside the footer; hub page "Policies & Disclaimers" linking every notice; "Last Updated" on the privacy policy.
