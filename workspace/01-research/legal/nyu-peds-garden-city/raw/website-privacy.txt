Websites Privacy Policy
Last Updated: February 27, 2026.
NYU Langone Health (“we,” “us,” “our,” “NYULH”) is committed to respecting your digital privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to personal information NYULH collects through our websites: https://nyulangone.org, https://med.nyu.edu, and https://medli.nyu.edu (collectively, the “Websites”). Personal information is information that identifies a specific individual.
This Privacy Policy only applies to the Websites and does not apply to personal information collected offline or on any other apps or sites, including sites otherwise maintained, controlled, or related to us.
Further, this Privacy Policy does not apply to protected health information that we process as a covered entity in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. For information about our practices related to protected health information, see our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.
Personal Information Collected
We collect personal information through the Websites in a number of ways, including directly from you, from cookies and similar technologies, and from vendors that support our services. For example, you may provide us, or our vendors, personal information when you visit the Websites, when you join a mailing list, when you donate, explore job openings, engage with prospective or current student or alumni content, or use interactive features.
Use and Disclosure of Personal Information
We and our vendors may use or disclose, including at your direction, personal information for various purposes, such as fulfilling your requests, providing the Websites, communicating with you, conducting surveys, marketing, improving our services, processing job or student applications, preventing fraud, supporting safety and security, enforcing our policies and terms, and complying with applicable law.
We may disclose personal information to our affiliates, business partners, and vendors. For example, we may use service providers to help us operationally or to administer activities on our behalf, such as sending emails, performing analytics, and providing interactive features on the Websites. We also may disclose your personal information as permitted or required by law or in the event of a business transaction (e.g., further to a merger, liquidation, reorganization, or any other business transaction, including negotiations of such transactions).
Cookies and Similar Technologies
We may use cookies, pixels, beacons, APIs, and other similar technologies on certain pages on the Websites. These technologies vary across the Websites and, depending on the type of technology and where it is located, may collect certain information about your visit to the Websites, such as IP address, details about your web browser and operating system, pages visited, time and length of visit, search queries, geolocation, and referring URL(s). These technologies may collect this information across the Internet and over time, and we, or our vendors, may combine information collected through these technologies with other information collected about you.
These technologies may be used for various purposes depending on where they are located on the Websites. For example, the technologies may facilitate performance or functionality of the Websites; provide features or other services you request (including location finders and interactive assistance chat features); gather analytics to understand usage and improve the Websites; provide job application interfaces (like allowing visitors to submit an application through a third-party service or account); help to combat fraud and support security; or be used for marketing purposes.
Your browser may provide options or settings that allow you to manage these technologies on that browser. You may also choose to use certain third-party browser add-ons or other solutions to adjust your preferences with respect to these technologies. We are not responsible for the opt-out functionality or settings tools provided by third parties, including their effectiveness. Please note that disabling certain technologies may affect your user experience on the Websites and some features may not function properly.
Data Security
NYULH has implemented reasonable physical, technical, and administrative controls to safeguard and secure personal information we collect online against loss, misuse, or alteration of the information under our control. Please note that any transmission via the Internet may be vulnerable, and NYULH cannot guarantee the security of information submitted or obtained through the Websites.
Links and Interactive Features
The Websites may contain certain links to other online services as well as interactive features provided by third parties that are not maintained or controlled by us. For example, the Websites may provide an interactive assistance chat or search feature that leverages artificial intelligence technology supported by our vendors. When you use an interactive chat or search feature, either with a virtual or live agent, the contents of the chat may be captured and kept as a transcript. Our vendors may process the information obtained through the feature to provide services on our behalf. Your use of these interactive features and links is voluntary. We do not control, and are not responsible for, services provided by third parties, and this Policy does not apply to online services not controlled or maintained by us, including sites or services you may access through links or embedded features on the Websites. Other online services may have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read before providing information on or through them.
Changes and Contact Information
We may modify this Privacy Policy in whole or in part at any time. Changes to the Privacy Policy are indicated by the “Last Updated” date at the top.
You may contact us about this Privacy Policy by emailing compliancehelp@nyulangone.org.
