## Flaws / risks
1. Stale dates: privacy and website terms Oct 2023, NPP June 2022 (verify with counsel against current HIPAA/state changes).
2. NPP is a 1.7 MB PDF with a misleading file name.
3. Privacy policy is California-first (16 mentions) with little New York content.
4. Under-18 exclusion is not suitable for a pediatric site (do not copy).
5. No accessibility statement, no nondiscrimination notice, no SMS terms page despite SMS marketing consent.
6. Mandatory arbitration/class waiver in website terms: heavy for a patient site (verify with counsel before any such clause).

## What to borrow
- Patient Billing Policy page and public cash-price service list linked from footer; billing answers in plain steps.
