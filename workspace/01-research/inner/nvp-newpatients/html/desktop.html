New Patients

Welcome to Northern Virginia Pediatric Associates, P.C., we are honored that you have chosen our practice. We care for newborns, children and young adults up to 21 years of age. Our goal is to offer your children the best possible medical care to help them mature into healthy adults. We want them to have healthy bodies and stable emotions in order to reach their full potential.

All our Physicians are board certified / board eligible, are members of the American Academy of Pediatrics, and are affiliated with Inova Fairfax Hospital and Virginia Hospital Center-Arlington.

Below is helpful information to assist you in becoming familiar with our Practice.

New patients must register prior to making an appointment.  Please contact your previous provider and request your child’s records be transferred.  Immunization records are needed prior to scheduling immunization or well visit.  Please be aware that medical forms need to be completed by the physician who preformed the last well visit. You can fill out the Patient Registration form either Online or by using the PDF version.

Patients can select their own provider, and most appointments will be made with that provider. If your provider is not available for a visit, you will be given the choice of waiting for your provider or having your child’s needs cared for by another provider in the group.

Please discuss with the receptionist what kind of appointment you need so that the proper amount of time can be allotted to you. More on Types of Visits.

The easiest and fastest way to reach us for non-urgent matters is through FollowMyHealth®, our secure patient portal. To set up your FollowMyHealth® account, visit our New Patient Portal
page.

For URGENT medical concerns, call our main number and press option 1. After hours, one of our providers is available for emergencies and can be reached by calling our main number. More on Urgent Line and non-urgent concerns.

Northern Virginia Pediatric Associates, P.C. is proud to have a high-quality laboratory. Our in-office laboratory is very convenient for patients and their families. More on Laboratory.

Our website is a wealth of information and we invite you to explore it. Please familiarize yourself with our Office Policies. More on Insurance & Billing.

Thank you for entrusting us with your child’s care. We look forward to serving your family in the coming years.

Your First Visit What you need to know for your first visit

FollowMyHealth® Patient Portal

FMH messages are reviewed M – F 8am – 5 pm (excluding holidays) and may take up to 72 hours to be returned.

For medical emergencies, call 911. For URGENT MEDICAL concerns, please call our office and select option 1.

Forms sent outside of a Well Visit will incur an administrative charge of $25 and may take up to 5-7 business days to be completed. When available and requested, forms can be expedited (24 to 48 hours Mon-Fri) and will incur a fee of $50 per form.
