    Pediatricians provide primary care for infants, children, and adolescents up to 18 years old. As a parent, you should follow the guidance below on routine checkups for your child.  Find a pediatrician.

    CHILDREN'S HEALTH TIMELINE

    Childhood Conditions and Our Care

    At AdvantageCare Physicians (ACPNY), our pediatricians will work with you and your children to assess and monitor their health and establish good habits at a young age. Our pediatricians can treat a range of childhood and developmental issues, including:

    • Acne and other dermatological conditions
    • Acute illnesses (e.g., sore throat, flu)
    • Asthma and childhood allergies
    • Behavioral disorders
    • Chronic illnesses (e.g., type 1 diabetes)
    • Constipation or digestive issues
    • Developmental issues
    • Ear infections
    • Respiratory conditions

    Our pediatricians can also perform a variety of routine exams and care, such as:

    • Immunizations
    • Back-to-school physicals
    • Annual wellness checkups
    • Early vision and hearing screenings

    Our Approach to Pediatrics

    Pediatrics and Whole You Care

    At ACPNY, we take a “whole you” care approach to treating your kids. Most of our providers have been practicing for over a decade, bringing a wealth of knowledge and expertise to the care they offer patients. We are committed to making sure your child develops healthy habits and is up to date on immunizations to lower the risk of childhood diseases. Plus, our pediatricians learn about the uniqueness of each family’s structure and dynamics, and will take these into account when developing a care plan for your child.

     

    Here When You Need Us

    In addition, we offer 24/7 phone access to pediatric advice through the University Hospitals Rainbow Advice Center, to make sure you can always reach someone to discuss your child’s health. Our practice also offers the myACPNY app, giving you immediate access to your child’s vaccine records, lab and x-ray results, and helpful educational resources.

     

    Care for New and Expectant Mothers

    ACPNY pediatricians also collaborate with our women’s health department on meeting with pregnant mothers seeking pediatric care within our practice. We even offer pre-natal interviews to establish lines of communication and answer your questions about what to expect. We know that as a new or expecting parent, you may feel anxious about the health of your newborn, but you can rest assured knowing you’ll have access to a team of knowledgeable, caring providers.


    To learn more about our pediatric services, check out our comprehensive suite of articles and videos on this page. To schedule an appointment, click here.
