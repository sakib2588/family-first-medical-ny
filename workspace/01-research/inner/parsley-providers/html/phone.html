Our approach to delivering quality care

Board-certified clinicians

All Parsley clinicians are MDs, DOs, NPs, or PAs board-certified in conventional medicine with advanced training in functional medicine.

Functional medicine fellowship

Each clinician completes Parsley’s intensive fellowship, plus advanced coursework through the Institute for Functional Medicine.

Evidence-based protocols

Parsley maintains the only complete, current, and fully documented set of evidence-based functional medicine protocols.

Advanced lab & diagnostic testing

Our clinicians can order and interpret advanced lab testing and specialty tests for deeper, whole-body insights.

Parsley's clinical collective

Your care is supported by a wide team of expert clinicians who share case review and leverage the latest science to build protocols.

Measurable results & progress tracking

Our proprietary Parsley Symptom Index (PSI) is a clinically validated tool to track symptom severity and measure progress over time.

A physician-led model that combines conventional and functional medicine
Parsley Health delivers functional medicine through a physician-led clinical model designed to be consistent, evidence-based, and deeply personalized. Our clinicians are board-certified and conventionally trained, with additional fellowship training in evidence-based functional medicine. Care is guided by shared clinical protocols and continuously refined through outcomes data, research, and physician oversight.
