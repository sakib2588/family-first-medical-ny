Welcome — We’re excited to meet you and your family!

Thank you for your interest in Tribeca Pediatrics! For over 30 years, we’ve been caring for kids across New York and New Jersey, and we can’t wait to welcome your family. Here’s why families choose us:

  • The best pediatricians and staff at local, neighborhood offices
  • 30+ years of experience caring for kids
  • 24/7 access to medical advice and guidance
  • Same-day and virtual visits, with weekend and evening hours
  • Patient portal with online scheduling, forms, and medical records access
  • In-network with most insurances and Managed Care Medicaid, including HIP, Healthfirst, UnitedHealthcare Community Plan, Fidelis Care, MetroPlus, Affinity by Molina, Empire BCBS HealthPlus, and more!

Getting Started

How do I join Tribeca Pediatrics?

Expecting?
Congratulations! You can register before your baby arrives using your expected due date. If the baby doesn’t have a first name yet, use “baby” as a placeholder. We will update everything once your little one is born. We always have appointments for newborns, so call us after you deliver and we will schedule your new baby.

New Patients
We would love to be your new pediatrician. New patients can register online using our Patient Portal. To transfer your child’s medical records from their previous pediatrician, use this Medical Release Form. We can also work with you to input previous records and vaccines.

Does Tribeca Pediatrics accept my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans and Managed Care Medicaid. We will never charge membership or concierge fees — our goal is to make quality pediatric care accessible to every family. You can view our full list of accepted plans on our Insurance page, or submit a photo of your insurance card and a member of our team will follow up with you directly.

What should I do before my child’s first appointment?

We will follow up with you after your child is registered. You can send medical records, immunization history, insurance card, and any necessary forms to patientcoordinator@tribecapediatrics.com.

Can I schedule a prenatal visit with a pediatrician?

Absolutely! We offer prenatal Info Sessions both virtually and in-person at select offices, so expectant parents and parents new to the practice can learn about Tribeca Pediatrics, meet one of our pediatricians, and ask any questions.
