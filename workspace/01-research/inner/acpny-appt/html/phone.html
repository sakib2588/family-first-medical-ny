    Same-Day/Next-Day Appointments

    1. Home
    2. Same-Day/Next-Day

    Looking to schedule an appointment with the soonest available AdvantageCare Physicians provider? Consider a same-day/next-day appointment at one of the ACPNY locations that offer this convenient option.

    Same-day/next-day appointments are for patients dealing with minor illnesses or injuries that aren’t severe enough to require immediate attention or a trip to the emergency room, like:

    • Strains and sprains, minor injuries, or rashes.
    • Cold or flu symptoms.
    • Discomfort or burning with urination.
    • Nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea.

    Click here, or click below to search for same-day/next-day appointment availability at one of these medical office locations:

    • Brooklyn Heights
    • Upper East Side

    If you’re trying to schedule a routine visit, physical, post-hospital discharge follow-up, medical clearance, or form completion, please call 646-680-4227 or click here to schedule an appointment with your Primary Care Provider (PCP) or find a new PCP.
