What are your wait times today in Astoria?

Ask Astoria for the current queue and booking guidance. Waits change with demand and clinical urgency; a fixed number or immediate rooming cannot be promised. Tests and procedures can take additional time after assessment. Severe symptoms need emergency assessment rather than waiting in a routine queue.

What conditions do you treat at your Astoria urgent care?

Urgent care assesses stable illnesses and minor injuries that need prompt attention, such as a sore throat, urinary symptoms, a minor cut or a sprain. The clinician reviews symptoms, examines you and decides whether tests, treatment or referral are needed. Confirm the selected clinic's age limits and specific tests or procedures. Ongoing disease management belongs with a regular primary clinician; severe or life-threatening symptoms need emergency care.

Do you offer COVID, flu, and rapid testing in Astoria?

Confirm the exact assay and collection service at the selected clinic. Antigen, molecular and combined respiratory tests have different uses and reporting intervals. The clinician should choose testing from symptoms, exposure and risk; a rapid test does not check every cause of illness or guarantee same-day treatment.

Do you provide UTI testing and same-day treatment?

Urinary symptoms need clinical assessment; a clinician may use urinalysis or culture and select treatment from history and risk. Fever, flank pain, vomiting or pregnancy changes the pathway and urgency. Confirm the selected clinic's testing and appointment capability; a visit does not guarantee a particular antibiotic.

Do you accept Medicaid and self-pay patients in Astoria?

Check the exact insurance product, clinician, location and service with both the office and insurer. A company name, Medicaid or Medicare alone does not establish that every plan is in network. Ask about the visit and test or procedure separately, your copay or deductible, and any referral or authorization requirement. Coverage is not a guarantee of no charge.

Do you provide school and sports physicals in Astoria?

Ask Astoria about an appointment for the exact school, sports or camp physical and the patient's age. Bring the required form and deadline. Confirm whether all exam and documentation requirements can be met there; a general urgent care booking does not establish a sports clearance visit or same-day signed form.

Is your Astoria urgent care convenient for nearby neighborhoods? Is your Astoria location easily accessible by public transportation?

Confirm Astoria's current street address and entrance before planning the trip, then check the transit operator's live route or parking information. A neighborhood description does not establish accessible entrances, parking availability or today's service. Ask the clinic directly about any mobility access requirement.

Do you treat children and adults at the Astoria location?

Confirm Astoria's minimum age and clinician availability for the exact concern before traveling; a general family-care listing does not establish every age is accepted. A baby age 3 months or younger with a rectal temperature of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher needs immediate medical advice and assessment. Severe breathing trouble, unresponsiveness or other severe symptoms require emergency help.

Urgent care and clinic access

Urgent care evaluates a new illness or minor injury that needs attention before a routine appointment. A persistent sore throat, painful urination, a small cut or a painful sprain may fit this setting. The visit starts with the problem you are having today and an assessment of whether a clinic can safely manage it. The clinician may arrange testing, treatment, a specialist referral or emergency care.

How urgent care and clinic access works

Your symptoms, vital signs and examination guide the next step. Testing is useful when it can clarify the diagnosis or change treatment. A respiratory swab cannot rule out every cause of a cough. A normal X-ray does not show every soft-tissue injury. Antibiotics treat particular bacterial infections and do not treat an ordinary viral cold.

Eligibility: Urgent care and clinic access

Choose urgent care for a stable, non-emergency problem. Use primary care for preventive visits and ongoing management of diabetes, blood pressure or other chronic conditions. Children need age-appropriate care. Call the clinic to confirm its current pediatric age limits and services before travel, especially for infants.

What to expect: Urgent care and clinic access

Describe when the problem started, how it has changed and what you have already tried. The clinician checks for serious causes, discusses the working diagnosis and explains the treatment or testing plan. Before leaving, ask who will contact you about pending results, what symptoms require reassessment and when to arrange follow-up. A visit does not guarantee a prescription, imaging or a completed clearance form.

Preparing for your appointment

Bring identification, your insurance information if you have it, a medication and allergy list, relevant records and any required form. Tell the team about pregnancy, immune suppression and recent procedures. If an injury involves a bite or puncture, bring the date of your last tetanus vaccine if known. Describe any medicine taken for fever or pain.

Risks and when to seek help

Call 911 for chest pressure with difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, severe breathing trouble, loss of consciousness, uncontrolled bleeding or a serious injury. Do not wait for a clinic booking. Sudden severe abdominal pain, a rapidly worsening illness or an infant with fever may need hospital assessment. An emergency can be present even when a home test is negative.

Follow-up and treatment review

Follow the written plan. Seek reassessment if symptoms worsen, new warning signs appear or you are not improving within the interval the clinician gave you. Arrange primary care for problems that recur or need ongoing monitoring. Ask how to obtain the visit summary and share it with your usual clinician.

Appointments, costs and coverage

Check the selected clinic's current hours and booking options. A walk-in option does not establish today's wait, capacity, test menu or holiday hours. Ask about the visit fee, separately billed laboratory or imaging services and your exact plan's network status. Confirm a particular procedure or vaccine before traveling.
