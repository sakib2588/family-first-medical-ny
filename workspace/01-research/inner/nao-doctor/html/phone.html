About Dr. Priti

Dr. Priti Jain, co-founder of Nao Medical in 2009, is dedicated to eliminating healthcare disparities by providing accessible, affordable, and community-focused healthcare services, particularly for underserved and marginalized groups. Inspired by her experiences in Bronx emergency rooms, she developed an innovative care model prioritizing patient needs, inclusivity, and community engagement, with a special focus on supporting working women. Under her leadership, Nao Medical has grown significantly, offering a comprehensive range of services and fostering diversity and inclusivity in healthcare.
