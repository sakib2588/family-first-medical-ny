Care where you are

Our virtual functional medicine care brings the same depth, attention, and human connection you’d experience in our centers, delivered seamlessly into your everyday life.

Virtual visits that feel personal

Meet one-on-one with your Parsley clinician and nutrition coach through secure video visits designed for unrushed conversations and real connection. Longer visits mean time to listen, ask questions, and understand your full health picture.

Advanced labs, coordinated for you

Access Parsley’s full suite of advanced lab testing from home. We coordinate at-home kits and nationwide lab draws, with clinician-led interpretation so results never live in a portal without context.

Virtual member events and education

Join live, members-only virtual events led by Parsley clinicians, covering topics like hormones, gut health, mental health, longevity, and nutrition. Learn, ask questions, and stay engaged from anywhere.

Personal supplements, delivered

Order clinician-recommended, medical-grade supplements through our online store with member-only pricing. Our care team is available to help with refills, questions, and adjustments.

Welcome in our centers

Virtual members are always welcome in our NYC and Los Angeles centers. Stop by to relax, use the space, browse supplements, enjoy a beverage, or attend in-person events whenever you’re in town.

Concierge-level support

From scheduling to supplements to labs, our care team is here to help. Virtual care comes with the same high-touch support and responsiveness as in-person membership.

“Not everyone lives near a center, and they shouldn’t have to. Our virtual care experience is designed to deliver the same depth, presence, and clinical rigor, so Parsley can be a steady partner in your health wherever you are.”

Dr. Robin Berzin, MDFounder & CEO

What virtual care looks like at Parsley Health

Our nationwide telehealth care is led by board-certified clinicians trained in functional medicine. We work with people seeking answers to persistent symptoms, deeper insight into their health, or a more proactive approach to long-term wellbeing.


Our care includes:

Longer, in-depth medical visits

Advanced lab testing and clinician interpretation

Personalized treatment plans

Ongoing care coordination and support

Flexible options to move between care, labs, and clinical review visits over time
