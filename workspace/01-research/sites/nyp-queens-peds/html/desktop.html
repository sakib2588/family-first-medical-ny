Pediatric Care in Queens

Find a location

Superior pediatric care, right here in Queens

When it comes to your child’s health, having access to expert care means everything. The Children’s Hospital of New York offers outstanding care for children with even the most complex and rare conditions. With services across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Westchester, and Hudson Valley, we are the children’s hospital in New York with the most pediatric specialists.* 

You’ll find this level of world-class pediatric care at NewYork-Presbyterian Queens, our hospital in Flushing, as well as several locations throughout the borough. As the Children's Hospital of New York, we provide children with a range of pediatric services, including general pediatrics, cardiology, neurology, gastroenterology, general surgery, orthopedic surgery, pulmonology, and urology, right in their neighborhood. Our pediatric experts are also equipped to care for the most high-risk infants, including babies with low birth weight, in the hospital’s designated Level III Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU).

*Based on registered physicians on Doximity, which represents more than 80% of physicians in the U.S.

Our approach to care

At the Children’s Hospital of New York at NewYork-Presbyterian Queens, your child will benefit from a multidisciplinary team of compassionate experts. Our pediatric team recognizes that every child with a medical condition has a unique set of symptoms, circumstances, and needs. We partner with families in shared decision-making, empowering them with knowledge, support, and the most effective treatment options. 

For children staying in the hospital, we offer a Child Life program to help ease the stress and trauma of illness and hospitalization. Our certified Child Life specialists work with you and your child before a procedure or surgery to address any fears, reduce anxiety, and answer any questions.

Theresa Lang Children’s Ambulatory Center

The Theresa Lang Children’s Ambulatory Center at NewYork-Presbyterian Queens provides convenient, comprehensive healthcare for infants, children, adolescents, and young adults. We are proud to be recognized as a Patient-Centered Medical Home (PCMH), a facility that designates a personalized care team of primary care and specialty providers to address all of your child’s healthcare needs. Our center offers same-day sick care, lab and imaging services, emergency care, well-child visits, chronic disease management, social services, community resources, and more.
