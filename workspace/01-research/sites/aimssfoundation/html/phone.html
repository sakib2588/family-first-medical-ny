Our Story

Communities carrying the heaviest burden often face the biggest barriers to care.

Led By
Samuel Michael, FNP & Carisol Michael, CHW

AIMSS was founded by Professor Samuel Michael, a family nurse practitioner and educator, and Carisol Michael, a health educator, in response to a persistent reality: communities carrying the greatest burden of chronic disease often face the greatest barriers to receiving timely, respectful, and coordinated care.

Through their professional and community work, Samuel and Carisol witnessed how Black, Brown, Hispanic/Latino, immigrant, and other underserved populations were affected not only by illness, but also by limited access to care, language and cultural barriers, inadequate health education, financial hardship, discrimination, and difficulty navigating a complex healthcare system.

They established AIMSS — Advocacy, Innovation, Medicine & Support Systems — to help change this reality: reducing health disparities and empowering communities through education, advocacy, prevention, care navigation, and community-based support.

Founders & Leadership

Two co-founders, one unfashionable belief about care.

Samuel Michael and Carisol Michael founded AIMSS on the belief that health equity is built one relationship at a time — in the clinic, in the home, and everywhere in between.

Samuel Michael, FNP

Co-Founder, President & Executive Director

A family nurse practitioner and educator, Samuel has spent his career at the bedside and in the classroom — watching, again and again, how the same diagnosis plays out differently depending on a patient’s zip code, language, or insurance status.

He leads AIMSS’s clinical and educational direction, translating frontline experience with transitional care, chronic disease, and specialty populations like sickle cell disease into programs built around what patients actually need.

Carisol Michael, MA, CHW

Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Carisol Michael is a public health educator and community health worker dedicated to improving how families access health information, preventive services, and support. She holds master’s and bachelor’s degrees in Community Health Education from the City University of New York (CUNY). Carisol began nursing school intending to become a registered nurse, then pursued public health as her interests in epidemiology, biostatistics, and community prevention grew.

Carisol spent a total of 10 years at NYC Health + Hospitals, including seven years in the pediatric department at Lincoln Medical and Mental Health Center. As a public health educator and program coordinator at Lincoln, she worked with clinicians and families on initiatives addressing nutrition, physical activity, asthma, diabetes, and chronic disease prevention. She implemented two programs funded by the New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, liaised with the department, and contributed to grant writing, fundraising, data tracking, and program management. She was also integral to fundraising for Lincoln Fit and Fun and carrying out its objectives for children and families. At Elmhurst Hospital Center, she built community partnerships and worked with public officials to advance public health initiatives.

Carisol later spent four years as a teacher with the New York City Department of Education. During the first wave of COVID-19, she helped her school implement remote learning and supported colleagues through the transition. She saw firsthand how technology access, nutrition, and other social determinants of health shaped children’s learning and family well-being.

As AIMSS’s Chief Executive Officer, Carisol brings her experience in healthcare and education to its mission of reducing health disparities. She helps build partnerships and programs guided by community voices, measurable needs, dignity, and culturally congruent care.

Board Members

Clinicians and advocates guiding our work.

AIMSS’s Board brings together decades of frontline clinical care, nursing education, and community advocacy to guide the organization’s direction and hold its programs accountable.

Manjit Saini, FNP-C

Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner

Manjit has cared for patients across New York since 2019, with experience in family medicine, primary care, urgent care, and pediatrics. Nationally certified through the AANPCB, he earned his medical degree in Ukraine and completed postgraduate training in radiology.

He focuses on preventive health and chronic disease management, and has served as a clinical preceptor for Nurse Practitioner and Physician Assistant students. Fluent in English, Hindi, Punjabi, Urdu, and Russian.

Puneet Kaur, PA-C

Board-Certified Physician Assistant

Puneet is a board-certified Physician Assistant with more than 15 years of experience spanning primary care, internal medicine, hospital medicine, and oncology. She holds a Master of Physician Assistant Studies from Touro University and graduated summa cum laude from St. John’s University.

She brings a calm, thoughtful approach to patients managing complex or multiple health concerns, shaped in part by her background in psychology. Fluent in English, Hindi, Urdu, and Punjabi.

Cassandra E. Dobson, PhD, MS, BS, RN-BC, FNYAM, CPH

Associate Professor, Lehman College · Co-Founder & President, Westchester Sickle Cell Outreach

Cassandra is an associate professor and director of the undergraduate program at Lehman College, CUNY. A nurse educator, researcher, and community advocate, she works to improve care and self-management support for people living with sickle cell disease and other chronic conditions.

Her career spans nursing leadership, patient education, and postdoctoral work in epidemiology and public health at Albert Einstein College of Medicine. Honors include the 2015 Iconic Women Award from the Sickle Cell Disease Association of America.
