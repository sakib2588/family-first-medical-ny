The doctors at NYU Langone Pediatric Associates—Garden City care for children from birth through age 20. We provide same-day appointments for sick children, and our doctors are available by phone anytime, day or night. Together, our team works to ensure short wait times in the office, and to give you and your child all the time you need with your child’s pediatrician. Our staff speaks both English and Spanish, and our certified medical interpreters provide on-site interpretation services.

Our care begins before birth, when we invite parents to meet us for a prenatal consultation. During this visit, we discuss any prenatal diagnoses and the care our expert pediatricians provide. As your child grows, we promote wellness from a holistic perspective and provide vaccinations, immunizations, well visits, developmental screenings, and sick visits.

Our highly trained specialists also provide comprehensive care for chronic conditions, including asthma, attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), and obesity. Our doctors work in close partnership with patients and their families to develop personalized treatment plans.
